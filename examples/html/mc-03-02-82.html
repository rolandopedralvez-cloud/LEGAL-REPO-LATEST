MC 03-2-82 — Identification of Amateur Club Radio Stations

Status
Active
Issued
1982-02-02

Pending revision of DPWC Department Order No. 13 (1971), authorizes the prefix DU or DX for identifying amateur club radio stations, or stations installed for a field trip or special event.

Call Sign Format and Transmission Requirement

Assigns prefix DU or DX followed by a figure denoting the amateur radio district and a suffix of up to three letters. The call sign must be transmitted at the start and end of each transmission, and at least once every ten minutes during transmission. Effective immediately upon approval.
