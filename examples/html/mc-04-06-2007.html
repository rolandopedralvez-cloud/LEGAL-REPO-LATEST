MC 04-06-2007 — Data Log Retention of Telecommunications Traffic

Status
Active
Issued
2007-06-01

Requires public telecommunications entities (PTEs) to retain call data records and non-voice traffic records (SMS, MMS, etc.) to support the prosecution of consumer complaints, implementing consumer protections under NTC MC No. 8-9-95 (IRR of RA 7925) and the Consumer Act (RA 7394).

Retention Periods

PTEs must retain traffic data (origin, destination, date, time, duration) for two months for non-metered fixed-charge services, four months for other telecommunications services, or until excused by NTC for records tied to a pending complaint.

Access and Enforcement

PTEs must provide NTC and complaining subscribers access to these records upon formal written request in connection with a pending complaint, furnishing hard copies within three days; records may not be released to others without a court order or subscriber consent. Non-compliance is subject to administrative and penal sanctions, and the Circular superseded inconsistent prior issuances, taking effect fifteen (15) days after publication.
