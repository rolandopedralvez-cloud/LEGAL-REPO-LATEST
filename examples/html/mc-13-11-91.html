MC 13-11-91 — Guidelines in the Issuance of the General Operator Certificate

Status
Active
Issued
1991-11-08

Pursuant to Act 3846 and ITU World Administrative Radio Conference provisions, and in preparation for implementing the Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS), promulgates guidelines for issuing the General Operator's Certificate (GOC).

General Qualifications

An applicant must be a Filipino citizen, at least 21 years old, of good moral character, must have attended an appropriate Inmarsat/GMDSS seminar or training offered by an accredited radio training center, and must be a licensed deck/radio officer.

Examination Competencies

A GOC is issued only to applicants who pass the NTC examination demonstrating detailed practical knowledge of Inmarsat and GMDSS subsystems, ability to send/receive by radiotelephony and direct printing telegraphy, and detailed knowledge of radiocommunications regulations and SOLAS provisions relating to radiocommunications. Effective immediately, superseding inconsistent prior issuances.
