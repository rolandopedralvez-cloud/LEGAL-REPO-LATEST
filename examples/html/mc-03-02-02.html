MC 03-02-02 — Clarification on MC 11-12-2001 (Spectrum User Fees)

Status
Active
Issued
2002-02-01

Clarifies Section 4.1 of Memorandum Circular No. 11-12-2001 (Spectrum User Fees), specifying that SUF rates cover both transmit and receive bandwidth combined, and provides a one-time deadline extension for that year's SUF payment.

Rate Interpretation

Clarifies that SUF rates cover both transmit and receive combined — e.g., PhP500,000/MHz means one MHz of both TX and RX combined, so 20 MHz total (TX+RX) totals PhP5,000,000 — applying this interpretation to all rates under Section 4.1 of MC 11-12-2001.

Payment Deadline

Extends the SUF payment deadline for that year only to February 2002, with the deadline reverting to no later than January 31 of each year thereafter. Took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
