Memorandum Circular No. 05-08-2005 — Voice Over Internet Protocol (VoIP)

Status
Active
Issued
2005

Addresses how Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) — a technology that blurs traditional boundaries between computers, telecommunications, and broadcasting — fits within RA 7925's framework, in keeping with the 1987 Constitution's recognition of the vital role of communications in nation-building and RA 7925's mandate to foster a healthy competitive telecommunications environment and a fair, efficient, responsive market.

Value-Added Service (VAS) Framework

Notes that RA 7925 introduced the concept of a value-added service (VAS) provider — an entity that, relying on the transmission, switching, and local distribution facilities of local exchange, inter-exchange, and overseas carriers, offers enhanced services beyond those ordinarily provided by such carriers. Under Section 11 of RA 7925, VAS providers do not need a franchise, provided they do not build their own network, since VAS is supplementary to basic telecommunications service rather than a public service offering in its own right. Because future value-added services cannot be fully predicted, and consistent with encouraging competition and innovation, "services ordinarily offered by local exchange and inter-exchange operators and overseas carriers" under RA 7925 must be construed strictly.
