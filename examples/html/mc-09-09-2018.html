Memorandum Circulars

Memorandum Circular No. 09-09-2018 — Rules and Regulations on the Selection Process for a New Major Player in the Philippine Telecommunications Market

Status
Active
Issued
September 2018, Quezon City, following a Notice of Public Hearing (August 8, 2018) and a public hearing held at the Novotel Hotel, Cubao, Quezon City on August 23, 2018

Establishes the rules governing the public, open, and competitive process for selecting a "New Major Player" (NMP) in the Philippine telecommunications market, in response to a presidential directive from President Rodrigo R. Duterte to the NTC and the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) to ensure entry of a new player offering the best possible services at reasonably accessible prices.

Background and Legal Basis

Cites Section 24, Article II of the 1987 Constitution on the vital role of communication and information in nation-building; Section 5 of RA 10844 (the DICT Act of 2015), designating DICT as the primary ICT policy and planning entity; and RA 7925 (the Public Telecommunications Policy Act), designating NTC as principal administrator. Notes that, per the 2017 annual reports of telecommunications companies, two dominant players held at least 40% market share each, and finds the entry of a new major player a matter of paramount national interest for genuine market competition.

Selection Process and Governing Issuances

Following the presidential directive, DICT issued Memorandum Order No. 001, series of 2018 (as amended), prescribing policy guidelines for NMP entry, and President Duterte issued Administrative Order No. 11 (April 6, 2018) creating an Oversight Committee for the NMP selection. The Oversight Committee and NTC determined that the NMP would be selected through a public, open, and competitive process among qualified congressional franchise holders that are not, and are not related parties to, the existing dominant players, using a "Highest Committed Level of Service" evaluation model. The Oversight Committee directed NTC to draft the implementing rules, which NTC finalized after evaluating public comments, recommendations, and position papers together with the Oversight Committee and the Philippine Competition Commission (PCC).

Section 1 — Definition of Terms

Defines terms used in evaluating an NMP applicant's committed service levels, including Capital and Operational Expenditure — the cumulative capital and operational spending in the Philippines directly related to installing, operating, and maintaining fixed and mobile networks and offering national and international telecommunications services, including broadband, calculated per applicable Philippine accounting standards (PFRS/PAS), covering both the initial cost of property and equipment and financing charges incurred during installation and construction.
