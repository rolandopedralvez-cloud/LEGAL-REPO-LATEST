MC 01-01-82 — Final Extension of VHF Equipment Deadline for Fishing Boats

Status
Active
Issued
1982-01-01

Grants a final, tiered extension of the VHF radiotelephone equipment installation deadline set under MC 05-02-81 (December 31, 1981), applicable specifically to fishing boats, after considering recommendations from the Philippine Coast Guard and the Federation of Fishing Associations of the Philippines.

Tiered Deadlines

All fishing boats utilized as “carriers” must be provided with the required VHF radiotelephone equipment not later than March 31, 1982. All fishing boats designated as “catchers” and small support craft must be so equipped not later than March 31, 1983.

Effect

The Circular explicitly frames this as a final deferment applicable only to the specified fishing-vessel categories, and took effect immediately upon approval.
