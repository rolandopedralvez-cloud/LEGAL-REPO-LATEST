Amending Sections 1 and 2 of Memorandum Circular No. 03-05-2007

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2018-08-28
Source/Authority
Act No. 3846, as amended; EO 546, s. 1979; RA 7925

Memorandum Circular No. 06-08-2018 amends Sections 1 and 2 of Memorandum Circular No. 03-05-2007 to add a formal definition of Ultra-Wide Band (UWB) technology and to prescribe the operating parameters and conditions governing UWB devices in the Philippines.

Definition of UWB

The Circular adds Section 1.16, defining Ultra-Wide Band (UWB), per ITU-R Recommendation SM1755, as a short-range radiocommunication technology involving intentional generation and transmission of radio-frequency energy spread over a very large frequency range, typically with a -10 dB bandwidth of at least 500 MHz or a -10 dB fractional bandwidth greater than 0.2.

Operating parameters

UWB devices are allocated the 6.0-8.5 GHz frequency band with a maximum mean EIRP of -41.3 dBm/MHz.

Operating conditions

UWB devices are subject to eight conditions: (a) transmission only while sending information to an associated receiver, ceasing within 10 seconds absent an acknowledgement and requiring a fresh acknowledgement at least every 10 seconds thereafter; (b) mandatory legible, indelible manufacturer/model markings; (c) no externally accessible control allowing operation outside specification; (d) a fixed integral antenna with no antenna connector; (e) optional interference-mitigating techniques; (f) operation on an unprotected, non-interference basis with no claim to protection and a duty not to cause harmful interference; (g) mandatory NTC type-acceptance testing for safety, EMC, and RF efficiency before use; and (h) a prohibition on UWB device operation on aircraft, ships, or satellites.

Effect on other issuances and effectivity

Any circular, memorandum, order, guideline, or rule inconsistent with this Circular is deemed superseded or amended. It takes effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and the filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
