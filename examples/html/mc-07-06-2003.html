MC 07-06-2003 — Additional Guidelines to MC 11-8-92 on Radio Operator Certificate Fee Collection

Status
Active
Issued
2003-06-25

Implements standardized fee collection procedures for radio operator certificates whose expiration dates are automatically adjusted to the holder's birth date, pursuant to MC No. 11-8-92.

Fee Proration and Validity

New radio operator certificates have validity automatically adjusted to birth dates. If the period between filing and the birthday is six months or less, the applicant pays 50% of the certificate fee; if more than six months, 100%. Certificates are issued for a maximum of three years, except GMDSS certificates which may be issued for up to five years. All other existing fees remain in force.
