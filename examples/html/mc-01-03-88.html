MC 1-03-88 — Procedure in the Purchase of Radio Transceivers from Abroad

Status
Active
Issued
1988-01-01

Addresses rampant unauthorized importation of radio transceivers by private individuals arriving from abroad, requiring valid NTC purchase permits in the traveler's name for acquisition of such equipment.

Permit Requirement and Confiscation

Only individuals holding a valid NTC permit to purchase, issued in the passenger's name, may acquire radio transceivers brought from abroad. Failure to present the required documents at arrival subjects the transceivers to confiscation under Republic Act 3846 and the Customs and Tariff Code.
