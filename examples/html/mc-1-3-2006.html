Memorandum Circular No. 1-3-2006 — Program Standards for Radio and TV Broadcast and Cable TV Stations

Status
Active
Issued
2006

Reaffirms NTC's continued recognition of the Kapisanan ng mga Brodkaster sa Pilipinas (KBP) as the self-regulatory body for Philippine broadcast media, and its adoption of the KBP Radio and Television Codes as NTC's program standards — a relationship formalized through the 1984 Instrument of Understanding, the 1991 Memorandum of Agreement, and the 1999 Memorandum of Understanding, and continuously adopted since NTC Memorandum Circular 3-4-85.

Rationale

NTC does not issue a new set of guidelines here, since the existing KBP program standards — together with NTC Memorandum Circular No. 11-12-85 and Memorandum Circular No. 22-89, which are likewise part of the KBP program standards — remain sufficient, widely accepted benchmarks for responsible radio, television, and cable broadcasting during normal times and during armed conflict, public peril, calamity, or other national emergencies. NTC supports KBP's strict prohibition on using broadcast facilities to advocate the overthrow of government by force or violence, or to propose or incite treason.
