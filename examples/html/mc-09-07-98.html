MC 09-07-98 — Guidelines for Issuance of Restricted Operator's Certificate

Status
Active
Issued
1998-07-20

In compliance with ITU Radio Regulations and the STCW 1995 Convention on GMDSS certification, promulgates guidelines for the training, qualifications, examination, and issuance of a Restricted Operator's Certificate (ROC) for radiotelephony aboard vessels operating within VHF coast station range.

Qualifications and Competencies

An ROC holder must demonstrate practical knowledge of GMDSS sub-systems within VHF range, ability to send/receive by radiotelephone, knowledge of radiotelephony safety regulations, and elementary command of an ITU working language. General applicant qualifications: Filipino citizen, at least 21 years old, of good moral character, having completed an accredited ROC training course, and holding a deck officer's license.

Examination and Fees

The ROC examination comprises Element 1 (Ship Radio Telephony theory) and a Code Examination (Morse alphabet transmission); a passing grade requires 70% in Element 1 (no rating below 50%) and 100% in the Code exam. An applicant who passes but fails to secure the certificate within one year must retake the exam. Fees: P50 examination fee, P150 annual certificate fee, P100 duplication fee.
