601 — Review and Allocation of Radio Spectrum

The National Radio Frequency Allocation Table (NRFAT) is reviewed every two years in the second quarter, and may be revised as necessary, or at any time where national security requires it, the Commission deems it necessary in the country's interest, or the Philippines has a regional or international commitment under a bilateral or multilateral agreement. Review and revision are conducted in consultation with industry and affected parties to optimize spectrum use. The NRFAT is a public document available upon request, and is kept consistent with the ITU's International Table of Radio Frequency Allocation while reflecting national priorities. The Commission allocates available frequencies to public telecommunications entities to satisfy projected customer access demand for the next ten years, based on justified target demand and radio frequency planning studies.
