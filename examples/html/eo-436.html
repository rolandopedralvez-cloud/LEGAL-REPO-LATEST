Executive Orders

Executive Order No. 436 — Prescribing Policy Guidelines to Govern the Operations of Cable Television in the Philippines

Status
Active — supplements EO 205, s.1987
Effective Date
September 9, 1997
Signed By
President Fidel V. Ramos

Sections 1–2 — Separate Undertaking and NTC Regulation

CATV operation is maintained as a subscriber-service undertaking with unique technology, distinct from telecommunications or broadcast television. Regulation and supervision of the CATV industry remains solely with NTC.

Section 3 — Authorization and Advertising

Only persons, associations, partnerships, corporations, or cooperatives with a Provisional Authority or Certificate of Authority may install, operate, and maintain a CATV system. Operators may carry paid advertisements and similar segments but may not infringe on broadcast television markets by inserting ads into carried/retransmitted programs without the program provider's consent.

Section 4 — Restriction on Related Services

Local exchange operators, broadcasters, and operators of direct broadcast satellite, multi-point distribution, TVRO program distribution, or similar video-delivery systems may not operate CATV systems or comparable video-delivery services without the specific permits/licenses required under this Order.

Sections 5–7 — Competing Authorizations, Leasing, Satellite Signals

NTC may grant a competing authority within the same franchise area of an existing Provisional Authority/Certificate of Authority (issued two years earlier) only upon finding non-compliance, grossly inadequate service, or that competition will not be ruinous to the existing operator. Operators may, with NTC approval, lease excess capacity to third parties. Receipt and distribution of encoded satellite program signals is limited to written authority from the satellite programmer.

Sections 8–10 — Implementation, Repeal, Effectivity

NTC is directed to implement this Order, adopt implementing rules, and impose penalties (including administrative fines and sanctions) after due notice and hearing. Inconsistent prior issuances are repealed or modified. Effective immediately upon approval.
