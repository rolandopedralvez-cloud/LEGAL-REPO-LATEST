MC 16-08-99 — Amendments to MC 10-07-98 Adding Schedule of Fees for GMDSS Radio Training Schools

Status
Active
Issued
1999-08-27

Amends Memorandum Circular No. 10-7-98 (STCW-95 implementation guidelines for radio training schools offering GMDSS courses) by adding a schedule of fees for certificate recognition, radio station licensing, and inspections.

Fee Schedule

Sets fees at: P150 filing fee for Certificate Recognition, P900 annual Certificate of Recognition fee, P150 application fee for Radio Station License, P300 annual Radio Station License fee, P400 Construction Permit fee, and P400 Annual Inspection fee. All other provisions of MC 10-7-98 remain in force, effective immediately.
