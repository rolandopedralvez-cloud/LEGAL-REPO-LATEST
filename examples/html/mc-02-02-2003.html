MC 02-02-2003 — Addendum to MC 17-12-2002 Allowing Non-Deck/ECE Attendance at RTC Courses

Status
Active
Issued
2003-02-12

Amends Memorandum Circular No. 17-12-2002 (Rules and Regulations Governing Radio Training Centers offering short-term GMDSS courses) to add a new provision allowing non-deck/radio officers or non-ECE applicants to attend RTC training, receiving only a Certificate of Attendance rather than exam-qualifying credit.

New Provision

An applicant who is not a deck/radio officer or Electronics and Communications Engineer (ECE) may attend RTC training but will only be issued a Certificate of Attendance upon completion, which does not qualify as a basis for taking the GOC or ROC examination. All other provisions of MC 17-12-2002 remain in force.
