Memorandum Circular No. 09-07-92 — Additional Guidelines in the Conduct of Restricted Land Mobile (RLM) Radiotelephone Operator's Seminar and the Issuance of the Corresponding RLM Certificate Within the National Capital Region and for Other Purposes

Status
Active — supplements MC 01-02-86 and MC 07-08-85/07-08-85A
Issued
1992

Adds NCR-specific procedures for conducting Restricted Land Mobile (RLM) radiotelephone operator seminars and issuing RLM certificates.

Request for Seminar

A seminar request submitted to the NTC-NCR Regional Office must include a construction permit, radio station license, or other authorization showing the assigned land mobile frequency; a list of qualified applicants certified as regular employees or members of the sponsoring agency/company/organization; four 2"x2" ID photos and a notarized information sheet per applicant for the RLM Radio Operator's Certificate application; and payment of a P10.00 filing fee and P20.00 application fee per applicant.
