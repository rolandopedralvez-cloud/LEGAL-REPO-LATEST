Memorandum Circular No. 06-06-2003 — Rules and Regulations Governing the Conduct of a Refresher Course by NTC Duly Accredited Radio Training Centers

Status
Active — implemented by accredited Radio Training Centers regulated under MC 16-12-2002
Issued
2003, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended, EO 546, Article 55 of the ITU Radio Regulations, and Regulation I/11 of the STCW-78 Convention, as amended

Governs refresher courses for GMDSS radio operator certificate holders conducted by NTC accredited Radio Training Centers.

General Provisions

A seafarer holding a GMDSS training certificate under the 1978 STCW Convention who has accumulated less than one year of relevant sea service in the preceding five years must attend a refresher course before taking the GOC examination. A GMDSS radio operator certificate holder in the same situation must attend a refresher course before receiving a revalidated certificate. Only NTC-accredited Radio Training Centers may offer refresher courses, and training certificates issued by non-accredited centers are not honored.
