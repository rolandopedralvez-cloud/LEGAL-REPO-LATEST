Amendment to Memorandum Circular No. 06-07-2016, Lifting the Suspension of the Implementation of the 4-Digit Exchange Codes for Area Code "02"

Memorandum Circular No. 04-07-2018 assigns specific Public Telecommunications Entity (PTE) identifier number ranges to carriers operating within Metro Manila's area code "02", implementing the migration from 7-digit to 8-digit local telephone numbers.

PTE identifier assignment

Existing 7-digit numbers migrate to 8-digit numbers (not 9-digit), with each PTE assigned a leading digit from "3" to "8" as its identifier prefix. Assigned ranges include 3000-3499 to Bayan Telecommunications, 5300-5799 to Eastern Telecommunications Philippines, 6000-6699 to ABS-CBN Convergence, the 7XXX block to Globe Telecom/Innove Communications, and the 8XXX block to PLDT/Digital Telecommunications Philippines, with several ranges (3500-3999, the 4XXX block, 5000-5299, 5800-5999, 6700-6999) left vacant for future assignment.

Migration procedure and deadline

Concerned PTEs must run public information campaigns starting three months before migration, maintain hotline support during the transition, and provide recorded announcements on the new format for three months after migration, using a common announcement design. All 7-digit numbers were required to migrate to 8-digit numbers no later than 31 March 2018, with new exchange-code requests subject to NTC approval.

Effect on other issuances and effectivity

Any inconsistent circular or order is deemed amended or superseded. The Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
