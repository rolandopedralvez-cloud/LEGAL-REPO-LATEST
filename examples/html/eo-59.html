Executive Orders

Executive Order No. 59, s. 1993 — Prescribing the Policy Guidelines for Compulsory Interconnection of Authorized Public Telecommunications Carriers

Status
Active
Signed
February 24, 1993
Signed By
President Fidel V. Ramos, by Chief Presidential Legal Counsel Antonio T. Carpio
Legal Basis
Republic Act No. 6849; Supreme Court decision mandating interconnection

This Order finds that the advancement of telecommunications is vital to nation-building and that enhancing competition among carriers, maximizing use of existing facilities, and giving the public access to telecommunications services nationwide at reasonable cost require the compulsory interconnection of all authorized public telecommunications carriers into a single, universally accessible, and fully integrated nationwide network.

Compulsory Interconnection

The National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) is directed to expedite the interconnection of all authorized public telecommunications carriers into an integrated network. Interconnection is defined as the linkage, by wire, radio, satellite, or other means, of two or more carriers' facilities and equipment so that subscribers of one carrier can access the services of, or otherwise communicate with, subscribers of the other. Interconnection between all authorized public telecommunications carriers is made compulsory.

Points and Manner of Interconnection

Interconnection must occur at local exchanges and trunk/junction exchanges with sufficient capacity to accommodate the traffic generated. Subscribers are given freedom to route their calls through the carrier access codes or equal-access arrangements of their choice. Interconnection extends beyond basic voice service to value-added services, including paging, trunking radio, and data switching networks.

Negotiation, Settlement, and NTC Intervention

Terms and conditions of interconnection are, in the first instance, for the concerned carriers to negotiate bilaterally. If the parties fail to reach an agreement within ninety (90) days, the NTC will intervene and determine the terms of interconnection. Technical standards for interconnection must conform to internationally accepted telecommunications recommendations. Traffic settlements between interconnecting carriers are computed on a per-minute, volume-based basis, with differentiated rates for day and night traffic.

Implementing Rules

The NTC is directed to issue the implementing rules and regulations of this Order within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

Sanctions for Non-Compliance

Carriers that fail or refuse to comply with the compulsory interconnection requirements of this Order, or with the terms determined by the NTC, are subject to administrative fines, suspension of pending applications, withholding of loan or financing accommodations, disqualification of responsible officers or employees, and/or suspension of authority to collect service rates, after due notice and hearing.
