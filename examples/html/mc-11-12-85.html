MC 11-12-85 — Revision of Program Standards (Public Service and Content Restrictions)

Status
Active
Issued
1985-12-11

Amends the Program Standards promulgated under Memorandum Circular No. 03-04-85 by adding new paragraphs “5” and “6” to item C, imposing public-service and content restrictions on radio broadcasting and television stations.

Paragraph 5 — Public Service and Honest Enterprise

All radio broadcasting and television stations shall provide adequate public service time, conform to the ethics of honest enterprise, and shall not use their stations for broadcasting or telecasting obscene or indecent language, speech, plays, acts, or scenes, or for disseminating false information or willful misrepresentation, or content detrimental to public health, or that incites, encourages, or assists subversive or treasonable acts.

Paragraph 6 — Duty to Cut Off Broadcast

All radio broadcasting and television stations shall, during any broadcast or telecast, cut off from the air any speech, play, act, scene, or other matter being broadcast or telecast if its tendency is to propose or incite treason, rebellion, or sedition, or if the language or theme used is indecent or immoral.
