MC 06-07-83 — Processing of Applications for Regular Examinations by Regional Offices

Status
Active
Issued
1983-07-17

Pursuant to the Decentralization Policy of Government, directs that applications for regular radio operator examinations be filed and processed within the Region where the examination is given, setting out procedures for Regional Offices in preparing master lists and coordinating with the Central Office.

Regional Office Duties

Each Regional Office processes examination applications for exams held in its Region, prepares a Master List of qualified applicants, and notifies them of the exam schedule and venue. At least two weeks before the exam date, the Regional Office forwards the Master List and examinee breakdown to the Central Office so the Examination Committee can prepare sufficient test materials.

Central Office Support and Transition

The Radio Operator and Training School Division (ROTSD) prepares a checklist of examination requirements and a fee schedule, with fees now collected by Regional Offices. Regional Heads must promptly communicate already-forwarded applications to the Central Office, and ROTSD continues processing pending applications during the transition to full decentralization.
