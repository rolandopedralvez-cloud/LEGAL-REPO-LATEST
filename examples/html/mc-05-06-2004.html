Memorandum Circular No. 05-06-2004 — Guidelines for the Grant of Authorization of Accounting Authorities in the Maritime Mobile Service

Status
Active
Issued
2004, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended, EO 546, and ITU-T Recommendation D.90 on charging and billing of maritime accounts

Defines the general duties and responsibilities of Accounting Authorities in the maritime mobile service, ensuring they operate consistently with the International Radio Regulations and applicable ITU-T Recommendations, particularly Recommendation D.90.

Definition of Terms

An Accounting Authority (AA) is an organization designated by the Administration to act as a billing intermediary between maritime mobile stations and service providers. Maritime Mobile Service covers the maritime mobile satellite service as well as MF, HF, and VHF radio media. Maritime Mobile Satellite Service covers mobile earth stations aboard ships, survival craft stations, emergency position-indicating radio beacon stations, and Inmarsat communications equipment. A Service Provider is a recognized operating agency providing communication service to and from maritime mobile stations.
