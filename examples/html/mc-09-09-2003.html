Memorandum Circular No. 09-09-2003 — Wireless Data Networks and Devices

Status
Active
Issued
September 9, 2003, pursuant to Act 3846 (Radio Control Law), EO 546 series of 1979, and RA 7925

Issued to promote and develop information and communications technology and facilitate the deployment, use, and development of wireless internet and wireless data networks and devices.

Section 1 — Scope

Covers all wireless data networks and devices operating within the 2400–2483 MHz, 5150–5350 MHz, and 5470–5850 MHz bands.

Section 2 — Definition of Terms

Defines Wireless Data Network as a network of computers and computing devices using radio frequencies; Indoor Equipment and Devices as radio equipment with effective radiated power (ERP) not exceeding 250mW and no external antenna (e.g., WLAN cards, Bluetooth-enabled phones and computers, personal digital assistants); Spread Spectrum Technology as radio communications using direct sequence or frequency hopping techniques; Outdoor Equipment/Radio Station as equipment exceeding 250mW ERP; and Effective Radiated Power as the power measured at the antenna output rather than the transmitter output.
