Memorandum Circular No. 14-11-82 — Implementation of the Revised Amateur Regulations

Status
Active
Issued
November 14, 1982

Implements Ministry Circular No. 82-077 (October 20, 1982), issued by the Ministry of Transportation and Communications as the revised Regulations governing the Amateur Radio Service, by setting out procedures and guidelines for effective implementation.

Accreditation of Amateur Organizations

An amateur association, club, or society seeking a Certificate of Accreditation must submit an application, a copy of its SEC certificate of registration and Articles of Incorporation, a membership list, and a certified list of board members and officers. Accredited organizations sign a memorandum of agreement with NTC for managing and supervising members' proper use of amateur frequency bands and preventing harmful interference.

Call Signs for Amateurs

Call signs use the prefix DU (Class A), DV (Class B), DW (Class C), or DX/DZ (club stations or field/special-event stations), followed by a district figure and a suffix of up to three letters. Call signs must be transmitted at the start and end of every transmission, and as frequently as practicable during the transmission, including tests and adjustments. Fixed station call signs must be posted at the station entrance in characters at least 10 cm high; mobile station call signs must be posted on the vehicle or vessel windshield (at least 5.08 cm high) and on the equipment itself (at least 0.635 cm high). An amateur wishing to operate for a special field trip or DX-expedition must first obtain a temporary permit from NTC.
