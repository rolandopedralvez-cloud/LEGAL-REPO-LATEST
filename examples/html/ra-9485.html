Republic Act No. 9485 — Anti-Red Tape Act of 2007

Status
Amended — later superseded in substantial part by RA 11032 (Ease of Doing Business Act, 2018), which is not itself in this repository
Enacted
June 2, 2007, Thirteenth Congress

Sections 1–4 — Policy and Coverage

Applies to all government offices and agencies, including local government units and government-owned or -controlled corporations, that provide frontline services — relevant to NTC's own permit, license, and certificate-issuing functions. Excludes offices performing judicial, quasi-judicial, or legislative functions. Defines "simple" and "complex" transactions based on the ministerial or discretionary nature of the required government action.

Sections 5–6 — Reengineering and Citizen's Charter

Frontline-service offices must undertake time-and-motion studies and re-engineer transaction systems to reduce processing time. Agencies must post a Citizen's Charter — detailing the procedure, responsible personnel, maximum processing time, required documents, fees, and complaint procedure for each frontline service — at the main entrance or in published materials in English, Filipino, or the local dialect.

Section 7 and Penalties

Heads of offices are primarily accountable for implementation and for rendering fast, efficient, convenient, and reliable service. The Act prescribes administrative and criminal penalties for violations such as fixing, undue delay, or failure to act on applications within prescribed periods.
