Memorandum Circular No. 03-08-2006 — Use and Operation of Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Within the 13.553-13.567 MHz, 918-920 MHz, and 2446-2454 MHz Bands

Status
Active
Issued
2006, pursuant to EO 546 series of 1979, Act 3846, RA 7925, and Rule 600 of MC 8-9-95

Issued in response to growing stakeholder interest — local and international — in Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology, whose widespread regional adoption reduces implementation costs through economies of scale, and in keeping with the ongoing review of spectrum allocation to keep pace with wireless technology developments.

Definition of Terms

RFID (Radio Frequency Identification Systems) are systems intended to carry data in transponders, commonly called tags, and retrieve that data by hand- or machine-readable means at a suitable time and place to satisfy application needs, with data in a tag providing identification of the tagged item.
