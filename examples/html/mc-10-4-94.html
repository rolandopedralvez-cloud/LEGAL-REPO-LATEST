MC 10-4-94 — Non-Exclusive Criteria for Certificate of Authority to Operate Cable Television Systems

Status
Active
Issued
1994-04-20

Sets non-exclusive criteria for evaluating applications for a Certificate of Authority (CA) to operate a cable television system, addressing service-area scope, minimum capitalization, and cable specifications.

Service Area and Capital Requirements

Limits proposed service areas to two municipalities per application/petition, except where municipalities are small and contiguous enough that operating in only two would waste resources. For CATV operations targeting 500 or more subscribers in their first year, the minimum capital requirement is not less than P1.5 million.

Technical Standard and Enforcement

Trunk cable must have a minimum diameter of 0.5 inch to remain compatible with future value-added services. The Secretariat and Broadcast Division are directed to strictly observe these requirements before accepting any CA application or petition.
