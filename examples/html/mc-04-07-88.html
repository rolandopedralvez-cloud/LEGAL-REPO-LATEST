Memorandum Circular No. 4-07-88 — Implementing Rules and Regulations Requiring All Radio Stations to Broadcast a Minimum of Four Original Pilipino Musical Compositions in Every Clockhour

Status
Active
Issued
March 1988, Quezon City, implementing Executive Order No. 255 (July 1987)

Implements EO 255's requirement that radio stations broadcast a minimum of four original Pilipino musical compositions (OPM) in every clockhour of a program with a musical format, in order to promote and popularize the local music industry and the nation's historical and cultural heritage.

Broadcast and Recordkeeping Requirements

"Original Pilipino musical composition" refers to any composition created by a Filipino, regardless of the language of the lyrics. Radio stations must log, for each musical-format program, the song title, the performing singer or band, and the time and date played.

Compliance and Penalties

NTC may require submission of records, logbooks, and other pertinent documents; direct stations to allow NTC representatives to examine program logs on-site; and monitor or record radio programs through authorized representatives or monitoring stations. A franchise holder or station operator that fails to meet the minimum OPM quota is fined P100.00 per violation, and NTC may, after due hearing, suspend or cancel the station's Certificate of Registration and Authority for repeated violations.
