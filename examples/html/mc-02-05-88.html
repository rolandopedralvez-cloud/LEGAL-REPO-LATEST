Memorandum Circular No. 2-05-88 — Rules and Regulations Governing the Manufacture, Acquisition, Sale and Service of Radiocommunication Equipment

Status
Active
Issued
1988, pursuant to the Radio Control Law (Act No. 3846, as amended) and Executive Order No. 546 (July 23, 1979)

Updates the rules governing the manufacture, acquisition, sale, and servicing of radio transmitters and transceivers in the Philippines, and establishes Radiocommunication Equipment Service Centers, enabling NTC to monitor and control transactions involving such equipment in the public interest.

Definition of Terms

Defines Radio Transmitter (a device emitting radio frequency waves for transmitting signals, messages, or intelligence), Transceiver (a combined transmitter and receiver), Dealer (a person or entity buying and selling transmitters/transceivers and their parts or accessories), Manufacturer, and Service Center Operator (engaged in repair, service, or maintenance of transmitters, transceivers, and other radiocommunication equipment).
