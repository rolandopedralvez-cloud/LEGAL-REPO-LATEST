Amendment to Memorandum Circular No. 4-09-88, Prescribed Equipment for Ship Stations of Various Vessels Plying Domestic Routes and Other Requirements, and Prescribing the Required Radio Operators Thereof

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2016-06-01
Source/Authority
Act No. 3396, as amended; EO 546, s. 1979; SOLAS Convention; RA 10635

Memorandum Circular No. 03-06-2016 amends Memorandum Circular No. 4-09-88's equipment and radio-operator requirements for Philippine-registered vessels on domestic routes, prescribing minimum radio installations, operator certifications, and distress/safety frequency-monitoring duties across nine vessel categories.

Equipment and operator requirements by vessel category

For each of nine vessel categories (Passenger Ships, Cargo Ships, Tankers, Tugs and Dredgers, Fishing Vessels, Yachts, Highspeed Crafts, Special Purpose Ships, and Miscellaneous Ships), the Circular prescribes minimum radio equipment and operator certification split by area of operation: bay/river/harbor/lake operation generally requires a VHF radio installation and at least a Third Class Radiotelephone Operator Certificate (3PHN) or Special Radio Operator Permit (SROP), while coastwise-voyage operation requires VHF plus MF/HF SSB radio (20-100 Watts) and at least a Second Class Radiotelephone Operator Certificate (2PHN), with GPS and/or AIS required above category-specific tonnage thresholds. The bay/river/harbor/lake certification requirement remains in force for five years from the Circular's effectivity and lapses once onboard equipment is upgraded to GMDSS, at which point vessels instead require at least two deck officers holding a General Operator Certificate (GOC), or one exclusive radio operator holding both GOC and Radio Electronic Certificate (REC). The exact tonnage thresholds and equipment specifications differ by vessel category; the full table in the verbatim text should be consulted for a specific vessel type.

Watchkeeping duties and distress frequencies

Licensed radio operators must keep authorized equipment operational and monitor designated distress and safety channels — including 2182 kHz, 4125 kHz, and 6215 kHz (HF/SSB distress), VHF Channels 6, 12, and 16, and the GMDSS DSC distress/safety frequencies (2187.5 kHz, the HF DSC frequency set, VHF Channel 70), NAVTEX (518 kHz), EPIRB (406/121.5 MHz), and SART (9 GHz) — and must maintain continuous radio watch during navigation per SOLAS Convention Chapter IV, Regulations 12 and 16.

Penalties and effectivity

Violations are subject to the penalties/sanctions under Memorandum Circular 4-89. The Circular took effect immediately upon issuance and superseded any inconsistent prior memorandum or order.
