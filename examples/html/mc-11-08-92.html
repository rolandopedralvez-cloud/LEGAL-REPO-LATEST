Memorandum Circular No. 11-08-92 — Adoption of New/Revised Certificate Forms for All Types/Classes of Radio Operator Certificates Being Issued by the National Telecommunications Commission

Status
Active
Issued
1992, pursuant to Act No. 3846, as amended, and Department Order No. 5, 1948, effective October 1, 1992

Adopts new and revised certificate forms for all radio operator certificate types except Restricted Land Mobile (RLM), to achieve efficiency, security, and simplicity in issuance.

Transition Procedures

Holders of certificates expiring on or before December 31, 1992 could file for renewal before that date, with validity running to the applicant's birthdate up to a maximum of three years. Holders of valid certificates could opt for early renewal by filing at least one month before their birthdate and surrendering the old certificate. Fees were charged yearly for the extension period (max three years), with no fee for a same-period old-to-new certificate re-issuance filed at least one month before the birthdate.
