MC 08-07-2007 — Frequency Relocation Bands for Legacy Users Affected by MC 06-08-2005

Status
Active
Issued
2007-07-19

Following the Commission's suspension of new frequency assignments in the 406-450 MHz and 470-512 MHz bands (proposed for reallocation to Broadband Wireless Access and other new services), allocates relocation bands for existing legacy users displaced by the reallocation under MC 06-08-2005.

Relocation Bands

Allocates the 406.10-410.00 MHz and 440.00-450.00 MHz bands exclusively for non-carrier services on a 12.5 kHz channel plan, as relocation bands for legacy users affected by MC 06-08-2005, forming part of the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table. Took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
