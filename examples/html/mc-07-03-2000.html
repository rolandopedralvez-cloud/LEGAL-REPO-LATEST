Memorandum Circular No. 07-03-2000 — Rules and Regulations Governing Shore-Based Maintenance

Status
Active
Issued
2000, pursuant to Republic Act No. 3846, Republic Act No. 3396, and Chapter IV Regulation 15 of the 1974 SOLAS Convention, as amended

Establishes shore-based maintenance requirements for Philippine-registered vessels equipped with Global Maritime Distress Safety System (GMDSS) equipment, and the establishment and accreditation of Radio Repair Service centers in the Philippines and abroad.

Purpose

Adopts standards and rules for shore-based maintenance requirements, enables NTC to monitor and supervise shore-based maintenance companies operating in the Philippines, and authorizes NTC to accredit shore-based maintenance companies and service centers operating outside the country.

Definition of Terms

Defines Radio Transmitter/Transceiver, GMDSS (a system using satellite and radio technology for immediate alerting, fast message distribution, and effective communications, especially during search and rescue operations), Shore-based maintenance service company (an entity engaged in repair, service, calibration, or maintenance of maritime radio communication and navigational equipment aboard Philippine-registered GMDSS vessels), and Accreditation of Shore-based maintenance company (the Commission's authorization for such repair/service/calibration/maintenance work).
