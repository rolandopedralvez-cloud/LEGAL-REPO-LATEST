Allowing the Use of Unassigned and Unused Allocated TV Broadcast Channels (TV White Space) on Secondary and Non-Interference Basis

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2017-04-18
Source/Authority
RA 7925; EO 546, s. 1979

Memorandum Circular No. 02-04-2017 permits the use of unassigned and unused VHF TV broadcast channels ('TV White Space') for broadband/internet access projects, on a secondary, non-interference basis relative to licensed TV broadcasters.

Rationale

The Circular cites the constitutional priority on nation-building communications infrastructure, RA 7925's mandate to extend basic telecom service to unserved areas, and figures showing that 87%, 48%, and 16% of public elementary schools, high schools, and health centers (respectively) lacked broadband/internet connectivity, as the basis for allowing TV White Space technology to deliver broadband access without interfering with licensed TV broadcast channels.

Use of TV White Space and NTC oversight

The Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) may use unassigned and unused VHF TV channels for government ICT projects, including the 'Free Wifi' project, strictly on a secondary and non-interference basis; TVWS stations must cease operation immediately upon an NTC Notice of Interference Report, and TV broadcasters retain priority such that any channel assignment to a broadcaster automatically recalls DICT's use of that channel. Only NTC type-approved/accepted equipment may be used, subject to prescribed fees. DICT's radio stations and equipment must be registered, permitted, and licensed, though spectrum use itself is exempted from spectrum user fees (registration/permit/license fees still apply).

Technical Working Group

A Technical Working Group (TWG) with representatives from DICT, KBP, and NTC is created within thirty (30) days of effectivity to review planned TVWS installations, which must be presented to the TWG at least thirty (30) days before installation; the TWG must decide within seven (7) days of presentation whether to allow the installation to proceed.

Effectivity

The Circular takes effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
