Executive Order No. 196 — Vesting the Jurisdiction, Control and Regulation Over the Philippine Communications Satellite Corporation With the National Telecommunications Commission

Status
Active
Effective Date
June 17, 1987
Signed By
President Corazon C. Aquino

Citing the need to supervise and regulate all satellite terminal stations delivering service to common carriers, and noting that the Philippine Communications Satellite Corporation (PHILCOMSAT) operates such facilities, this Order places PHILCOMSAT — including all its facilities, services, and rate-fixing — under the jurisdiction, control, and regulation of the National Telecommunications Commission. All inconsistent laws, orders, rules, and regulations are repealed or modified accordingly. Effective immediately.
