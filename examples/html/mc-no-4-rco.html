MC No. 4 (RCO, 1972) — Suspending Processing of Radio Station Applications Under Martial Law

Status
Superseded
Issued
1972-09-25

Issued by the Radio Control Office of the then-Department of Public Works and Communications on September 25, 1972 — four days after President Marcos's Proclamation No. 1081 declaring martial law — this Circular temporarily suspends acceptance and processing of applications for radio station construction permits, radio station licenses, modification of radio station licenses, and manufacturer's/dealer's permits, citing the state of emergency.

Suspension and Exemptions

In view of the state of emergency and martial law declared under Proclamation No. 1081 (September 21, 1972), the Radio Control Office suspends, effective immediately, acceptance and processing of applications for radio station construction permits, radio station licenses, modification of licenses, and manufacturer's and dealer's permits, until further orders. Exempted are renewals of radio station licenses and permits to sell, possess, own, and purchase radio communication equipment.

Service-Specific Exemptions

Applications for radio station construction permits and for permits to possess, own, transfer, purchase, and sell radio transmitters and transceivers are also exempted for: aeronautical stations, aeronautical fixed stations, aircraft stations, coastal stations, and ship stations. All prior memoranda or circulars in conflict with this Circular are deemed superseded or rescinded. A handwritten note on the original indicates this Circular was partly amended by Memorandum Circular No. 13, dated October 12, 1972.
