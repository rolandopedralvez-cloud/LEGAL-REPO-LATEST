MC 02-02-2004 — Ocular Inspections of Radio Stations Operated by Public Telecommunications Entities

Status
Active
Issued
2004-02-13

Pursuant to Act 3846 and RA 7925, sets guidelines for ocular and physical inspections of radio stations, cell base stations, and links operated by Public Telecommunications Entities (PTEs), to ensure operation within licensed limits and prevent harmful interference.

Inspection Procedure

Inspections are conducted only by NTC personnel holding a valid Mission Order signed by a Regional Director, Commissioner, or Deputy Commissioner, with law enforcement assistance available as needed provided an NTC representative is always present. PTEs must allow such personnel to inspect their radio stations, cell base stations, and links.

Reporting and Enforcement

Regional Directors must strictly observe their geographic areas of responsibility and submit written inspection reports to the Commissioner within five working days. Violations are dealt with in accordance with law. Took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
