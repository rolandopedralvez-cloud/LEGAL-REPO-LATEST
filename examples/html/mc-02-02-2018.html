Additional Frequency Allocations for Broadband Wireless Access (BWA)

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2018-02-09
Source/Authority
Act No. 3846, as amended; RA 7925; EO 546, s. 1979

Memorandum Circular No. 02-02-2018 re-allocates the 3300-3400 MHz frequency band for Broadband Wireless Access (BWA), aligning the Philippines' National Radio Frequency Allocation Table with the international identification of that band for IMT under the ITU's World Radiocommunication Conference 2015 (WRC-15).

Background

The Circular recites the Commission's mandate under RA 7925 (Public Telecommunications Policy Act) to keep spectrum allocation responsive to developments in wireless technology and to ensure wider, cost-effective access to the limited radio spectrum, and notes that the Philippines joined other countries in identifying the 3300-3400 MHz band for International Mobile Telecommunications (IMT) under Footnote 5.429F of the ITU Radio Regulations, as adopted at WRC-15.

Allocation

Pursuant to RA 7925, Act No. 3846 (Radio Control Law), as amended, and Executive Order No. 546, series of 1979, the Commission re-allocates the 3300-3400 MHz band for Broadband Wireless Access (BWA), which becomes part of the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table (NRFAT). Any circular, memorandum, order, guideline, or rule inconsistent with this allocation is deemed superseded or amended.

Effectivity

The Circular takes effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and the filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
