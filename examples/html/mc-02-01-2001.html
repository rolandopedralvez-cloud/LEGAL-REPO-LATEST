Memorandum Circular No. 02-01-2001 — Revised Customer Premises Equipment Interface Standards and Procedures for Type Approval and Type Acceptance

Status
Active — revises MC 1-04-88
Issued
2001

Updates the Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) interface standards and type approval/type acceptance procedures originally set by MC 1-04-88, in line with ITU-T and ITU-R recommendations.

Section I — Type Approval and Type Acceptance

Type approval evaluates a CPE for conformance to national interface standards through laboratory testing, ensuring the equipment will not harm end-users, the public network, or other spectrum users through malfunction or interference. Where a CPE has already been certified by a foreign approval authority, NTC or its accredited test laboratory may accept a manufacturer's self-declaration and foreign test reports and approval certificates in lieu of local testing, provided compliance with the prescribed CPE interface standards is shown.

Sections II–VIII — Scope, Interface Parameters, and Administrative Procedures

Sets scope and limitations, interface parameters, and test procedures for CPE, along with administrative procedures for type approval or acceptance, provisions for grandfathered CPEs already in service, a repealing clause for inconsistent issuances, and technical annexes covering electrical safety, electromagnetic compatibility, and network general requirements for specific equipment categories — corded and cordless telephone sets, PABX and key telephone systems (KTS), Cellular Mobile Telephone System (CMTS) and public mobile telephone terminals, fixed wireless access subscriber terminals, wireless paging equipment, data communications equipment (DCE/DTE), and answering machines.
