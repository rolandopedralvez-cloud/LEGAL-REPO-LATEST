MC 12-09-2004 — Amendments to MC 17-2-2002 on RTC Deficiency Correction Procedures

Status
Active
Issued
2004-09-24

Amends Section 17.3 of Memorandum Circular No. 17-2-2002 (Rules and Regulations Governing Radio Training Centers offering short-term GMDSS courses) and adds a new Section 17.4, setting procedures for correcting noted RTC deficiencies.

Amended Grace Period Procedure

An RTC with noted deficiencies is given a 30-day grace period from notice to correct them and inform NTC of compliance, after which the Commission conducts a re-inspection.

New Enforcement Provision

If an RTC fails to correct deficiencies within the prescribed period, the violation report is forwarded to the Legal Department for appropriate action, and the RTC is enjoined from accepting new students until the matter is resolved with finality. All other provisions of MC 17-2-2002 remain in force.
