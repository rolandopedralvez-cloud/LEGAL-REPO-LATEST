MC 08-04-99 — GMDSS Equipment Maintenance Requirements

Status
Active
Issued
1999-04-12

Pursuant to the 1974 SOLAS Convention as amended, requires specified maintenance methods for Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS) radio equipment aboard Philippine-registered vessels, calibrated to the vessel's sea area of navigation.

Maintenance Methods

Three recognized maintenance methods are: duplication of equipment; shore-based maintenance; and at-sea electronic maintenance capability. Vessels in sea areas A1 and A2 must ensure availability using any one of these methods; vessels in sea areas A3 and A4 must use a combination of at least two.

Personnel and Company Requirements

A person performing at-sea electronic maintenance must hold a General Operator Certificate (GOC) and a First Class Radio Electronic Certificate (REC), and shore-based maintenance companies must be registered with the Commission. Effective immediately.
