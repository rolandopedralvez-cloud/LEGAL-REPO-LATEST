Further Amending Section 2 of Memorandum Circular No. 03-05-2007

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2017-06-07
Source/Authority
Act No. 3846, as amended; EO 546, s. 1979; RA 7925

Memorandum Circular No. 03-06-2017 further amends Section 2 (Operating Parameters) of Memorandum Circular No. 03-05-2007, updating the technical limits applicable to certain short-range devices (SRDs), including non-specific short-range devices, telemetry, telecommand, alarm, and general data applications.

Amendment

Pursuant to Act No. 3846 (Radio Control Law), Executive Order No. 546, series of 1979, and RA 7925 (Public Telecommunications Policy Act), and in order to keep pace with developments in wireless technology while ensuring wider, cost-effective access to the limited radio spectrum, Section 2 of Memorandum Circular No. 03-05-2007 is amended to revise the operating-parameter table for the SRD categories it covers. The source scan of this Circular's table is only partially legible; the verbatim text below preserves what can be read directly, including frequency-band figures in the 868-870 MHz range for non-specific short-range devices, telemetry, telecommand, alarms, and similar data applications with field-strength/output-power limits in the low milliwatt range. Readers needing the complete table should consult the original source document.

Effect on other issuances and effectivity

Any circular, memorandum, order, or part thereof inconsistent with this Circular is deemed amended or superseded. It takes effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and the filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
