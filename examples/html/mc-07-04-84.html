Memorandum Circular No. 07-04-84 — Lifetime Amateur Radio Station License

Status
Active — amends Ministry Memorandum Circular No. 82-077 (Revised Amateur Radio Regulations, October 20, 1982)
Issued
1984, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended

Authorizes NTC to grant lifetime radio station licenses to qualified and deserving radio amateur operators, in recognition of their invaluable, continued, and faithful service to the advancement of amateur radio in the Philippines.

General Qualifications

An applicant must be at least 70 years old, have rendered at least 15 consecutive years of meritorious amateur service from the application filing date, and hold a valid Class "A" amateur station license.

Licensing Requirements

Requires a written request for the lifetime license, a certificate of good standing from a registered amateur club or association, a copy of the birth certificate, the original current radio station license, proof of the required years of amateur service, and payment of a P50.00 grant license fee.
