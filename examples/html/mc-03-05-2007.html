Memorandum Circular No. 03-05-2007 — Frequency Bands for the Use and Operation of Short Range Devices (SRDs)

Status
Active
Issued
2007, pursuant to RA 7925, Rule 600 of MC 8-9-95 (IRR of RA 7925), EO 546 series of 1979, and Act 3846 as amended

Issued in recognition of increasing worldwide demand for low-powered short range devices (SRDs) — used independently or as part of other systems, and often carried across national borders — and the need to review spectrum allocation to keep pace with wireless technology developments and ensure wider, cost-effective access to limited radio spectrum.

Allocation, Use, and Interference

Establishes rules for the allocation of frequencies for, and the use and operation of, Short Range Devices. Because SRD operational radio parameters vary widely across applications, SRDs cannot claim protection against interference from other radio services operating in the same bands.
