Memorandum Circular No. 04-03-2000 — Guidelines on the Configuration of the Reserve Source or Sources of Energy Used to Supply Radio Installations on GMDSS Ships

Status
Active
Issued
2000, pursuant to Regulation 4/13 of the 1974 SOLAS Convention, as amended

Requires ships to maintain, at all times while at sea, a sufficient electrical energy supply to operate radio installations and charge reserve batteries.

Reserve Power Requirements

Every ship must have a reserve source of energy to supply radio installations for distress and safety communications if the main and emergency power sources fail. The reserve source must simultaneously operate the VHF radio installation, plus either the MF, MF/HF radio installation, or the INMARSAT ship earth station, for at least one hour on ships built on or after February 1, 1995; one hour on ships built before that date if a self-contained emergency power source is provided; or six hours on ships built before that date without a self-contained emergency power source.
