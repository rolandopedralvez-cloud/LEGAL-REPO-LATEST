MC 07-07-2007 — Additional Frequency Band Allocation, 410-430 MHz for Broadband Wireless Access

Status
Active
Issued
2007-07-19

Pursuant to RA 7925, EO 546, and Act 3846, reallocates the 410-430 MHz band for broadband wireless access in addition to the bands previously allocated under MC 06-08-2005.

Reallocation and Effect

Adds 410-430 MHz to the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table for broadband wireless access. Transfer of previously authorized stations within the band is governed by Rule 603 of MC 3-3-96. Superseded inconsistent circulars and took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
