MC 07-04-99 — Exempting Unmanned Radio Stations from Licensed Operator Requirement

Status
Active
Issued
1999-04-12

Pursuant to Act 3846, EO 546, and RA 7925, exempts unmanned radio stations from the requirement of duly licensed radio operators, subject to engineering supervision and reporting conditions.

Conditions for Exemption

Unmanned stations must be operated and maintained under the direct supervision of a duly registered Electronics Communications Engineer (ECE); their valid radio station licenses must be posted conspicuously; and any service stoppage/interruption must be reported to the Commission within 24 hours, with restoration within 72 hours.

Definition and Effect

"Unmanned radio stations" means stations whose operation requires no human intervention except turning the power supply on and off. Superseded inconsistent prior circulars and took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
