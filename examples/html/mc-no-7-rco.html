MC No. 7 (RCO, 1972) — Procedure for Purchase, Sale, Supply, or Grinding of Crystals

Status
Superseded
Issued
1972-10-12

Issued by the Radio Control Office on October 12, 1972 pursuant to Letter of Instruction No. 13-A, requires a permit from the Radio Control Office before any person, company, or firm may purchase, sell, supply, or grind transmitter crystals, and sets out the permit procedure.

Permit Procedure

An applicant must accomplish an application form available at the Radio Control Office in Quezon City or its field offices in Cebu City, Davao City, and Tolosa (Leyte); for the Armed Forces of the Philippines, requests are submitted to the Chief Communications Electronics Officer, GHQ, AFP, who is authorized to issue the permit. After securing the permit, the applicant presents it to the dealer, supplier, or manufacturer, who then issues the authorized crystal; the buyer must submit a duplicate official receipt to the Radio Control Office afterward, and the dealer/supplier/manufacturer must reflect the sale in a monthly report.

Enforcement

Failure to comply subjects the buyer to the provisions of Act 3846, as amended, and the seller to revocation of its permit.
