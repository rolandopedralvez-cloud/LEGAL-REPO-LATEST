Interconnection Charge for Short Messaging Services and Voice Service

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2018-07-09
Source/Authority
RA 7925; EO 546, s. 1979; DICT Department Order No. 002, s. 2018

Memorandum Circular No. 05-07-2018 reduces the mandated interconnection charges between telecommunications carriers to PHP0.50 per minute for voice calls and PHP0.05 per SMS, citing comparatively high domestic rates against ASEAN benchmarks and a DICT directive to bring down consumer costs.

Background

The Circular traces the prior reductions under MC 02-10-2011 and MC 09-11-2016 (which brought SMS interconnection from PHP0.35 to PHP0.15 and voice from PHP4.00 to PHP2.50 per minute), and compares Philippine rates against India, Australia, and several ASEAN countries' mobile termination rates (ranging from no charge in Singapore, Myanmar, and Brunei up to PHP2.42 per minute in Cambodia), alongside NTC's own cost study putting average interconnection cost at PHP0.486 per minute for voice and PHP0.044 per SMS. It also cites DICT Department Order No. 002, series of 2018, directing NTC to bring interconnection rates to a minimum consistent with consumer interest.

New interconnection charges and obligations

The Circular sets the interconnection charge at PHP0.50 per minute for voice service and PHP0.05 per SMS. Network providers must comply with prescribed quality-of-service standards, and each interconnecting party must provide sufficient-capacity links and facilities for their respective traffic. Public telecommunications entities (PTEs) must amend their interconnection agreements within ten (10) days of effectivity, with the new charge to take effect no later than twenty (20) days after the Circular's effectivity.

Penalties and effectivity

Violations are penalized under existing laws, rules, and regulations. The Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified copies with the UP Law Center.
