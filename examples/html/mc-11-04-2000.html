MC 11-04-2000 — Amendment to Age Qualification for NTC Licensure Examinations

Status
Active
Issued
2000-04-14

Pursuant to ITU Radio Regulations and the 1995 STCW Convention, amends the age qualification for applicants to take the GOC, ROC, and REC licensure examinations under MC Nos. 13-11-91, 09-07-98, and 06-06-98, lowering the minimum age to 20.

Amended Age Requirement

Sets the minimum age qualification for GOC, ROC, and REC licensure examination applicants at 20 years old, with all other provisions of MC Nos. 13-11-91, 09-07-98, and 06-06-98 remaining in force.
