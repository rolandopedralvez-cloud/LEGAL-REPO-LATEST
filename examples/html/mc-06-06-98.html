Memorandum Circular No. 6-6-98 — Guidelines in the Issuance of a Radio Electronic Certificate (REC)

Status
Active
Issued
1998, pursuant to Act No. 3846, as amended, EO 546, Section IV-2 of the STCW Convention of 1995, and Article 55 of the ITU Radio Regulations

Sets guidelines for the training, qualification, examination, and issuance of a Radio Electronic Certificate (REC), related to the Certificate of Competency processing rules under MC 15-12-2002.

Qualification Requirements

An REC holder must pass the required NTC examination and demonstrate knowledge and competence in the principles of electricity and radio/electronics theory per ITU Radio Regulation Article 55, Section IIIA; theoretical knowledge of GMDSS radiocommunication equipment (narrow-band direct-printing telegraph and radiotelephone transmitters and receivers, digital selective calling equipment, ship earth stations, emergency position-indicating radiobeacons, marine antenna systems, and survival craft radio equipment); and practical knowledge of operating, preventive maintenance, and fault repair of such equipment using appropriate testing tools.
