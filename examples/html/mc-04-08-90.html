Memorandum Circular No. 04-08-90 — Sub-Allocation to Frequency Band 430-440 MHz

Status
Repealed — the Commission proposed rescinding this Circular in MC 10-05-90 after strong objections from the amateur radio community
Issued
February 6, 1990

Sub-allocates the 430-440 MHz frequency band, previously reserved exclusively for Amateur Radio use, opening most of the band to Fixed/Mobile (FX/ML) commercial services consistent with International Radio Regulations.

Sub-Allocation Table

Amateur use is retained only in 430.000–432.000 MHz and 435.000–435.500 MHz; the remaining ranges (432.000–435.000, 435.500–437.000, 437.000–437.500, 437.500–438.000, and 438.000–440.000 MHz) are allocated to FX/ML, FX/ML (broadcast), and FX/ML (Personal Radio Service) uses.
