Memorandum Order No. 10-10-2017 — Public Telecommunication Entity (PTE) Identifier Within Local Exchange Area Code "02"

Status
Active, pending amendment to Memorandum Circular No. 06-07-2016
Issued
October 2017, Quezon City

Issued pursuant to RA 7925 and EO 546 series of 1979 to address an insufficient number of assignable exchange codes in area code "02," building on Memorandum Circular No. 04-06-98 (adoption of 4-digit exchange codes for Area Code "2") and Memorandum Circular No. 06-07-2016 (lifting the suspension of that implementation), following a meeting with PTE representatives from ABS-CBN Convergence, ETPI, Globe Telecom/Bayantel, and PLDT.

Numbering Migration and PTE Identifiers

All existing seven-digit telephone numbers within the "02" local exchange area are migrated to eight-digit numbers (not nine digits), with each PTE assigned an identifier digit (3 through 8) prefixed to the existing three-digit exchange code: Bayan Telecommunications gets 3000–3499; 3500–3999 and the 4xxx and 5xxx ranges are left vacant; ABS-CBN Convergence gets 6000–6699; Eastern Telecommunications Philippines/Telecommunications Technologies Philippines gets 6700–6999; Globe Telecom/Innove Communications gets the 7xxx range; and PLDT/Digital Telecommunications Philippines gets the 8xxx range.

Implementation Requirements

Concerned PTEs must conduct public information campaigns at least three months before migration begins (via bill inserts, call-outs, broadcast, print, and social media), maintain a hotline during migration, advise subscribers via recorded announcements for three months after migration using a common recorded announcement format, and complete the migration to eight-digit numbers within ten months of the Order's effectivity. New PTE identifier requests are subject to Commission approval.
