Memorandum Circular No. 10-7-93 — Implementing Guidelines on the Domestic Satellite Communications Policy

Status
Active
Issued
1993, in line with Department Circular No. 93-273 and pursuant to EO 205, EO 546, Act No. 3846 as amended, and Act No. 146 as amended

Establishes guidelines to govern Domestic Satellite Communications Services in the Philippines.

Article I — Definition of Terms

Defines Satellite Communications as the transmission, emission, or reception of radio signals involving one or more space and earth stations; Satellite Earth Station and Space Segment (the orbiting satellite and associated tracking/control equipment, excluding ground equipment); Space Segment Provider (the owner/operator of an in-orbit satellite system that leases or sells capacity); Satellite Communications Service Provider (authorized to provide satellite services as a carrier's carrier and/or common carrier); Space Segment Lessee; Receive-Only Satellite Earth Station (for receiving data such as stock prices, newswire feeds, or television programs); Redistribution of Satellite TV Programs (retransmitting received satellite TV signals by any mode for public viewing); Satellite Television Receive Only (TVRO) Station; and Non-Commercial TVRO (NC-TVRO) Station, covering personal/family use and use by government agencies and instrumentalities.
