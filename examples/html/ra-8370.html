Republic Act No. 8370 — Children's Television Act of 1997

Status
Active
Enacted
July 28, 1997, Tenth Congress

Recognizes the vital role of youth in nation-building and the impact of broadcast media, particularly television, on children's value formation and intellectual development. The State commits to supporting television programs that reflect children's needs without exploiting them.

Sections 4–5 — National Council for Children's Television

Establishes the National Council for Children's Television (NCCT), attached to the Office of the President for policy coordination, composed of five members appointed on the basis of integrity and expertise in child development, broadcasting, or education, serving until successors are appointed. The Council organizes its own secretariat and an Advisory Committee to assist in formulating and implementing children's television policy.

Relation to Other Broadcast Content Rules

Complements existing broadcast content requirements such as EO 255's Original Pilipino Music quota, extending the State's programming-content policy interest specifically to children's television.
