MC 09-11-2005 — Frequency Allocation for Telemetry and Other Similar Data Systems

Status
Active
Issued
2005-11-23

Pursuant to RA 7925, EO 546, and Act 3846, reallocates the 481.250-481.475 MHz and 486.250-486.475 MHz bands for Telemetry and Other Similar Data Systems as part of the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table (NRFAT), after the previously allocated bands for these services were reassigned to the Public Radio Network Service.

Reallocated Bands and Channel Plan

Allocates 481.250-481.475 MHz and 486.250-486.475 MHz for Telemetry and Other Similar Data Systems, with an attached annex specifying 12.5 kHz and 25 kHz duplex and simplex channel plans for the band.

Effect

Forms part of the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table; supersedes inconsistent prior circulars, orders, or memoranda; took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three copies with the UP Law Center.
