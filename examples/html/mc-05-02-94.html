MC 5-2-94 — Registration and Licensing of Television Receive Only (TVRO) Stations

Status
Active
Issued
1994-02-08

Pursuant to Department Circular No. 93-273 as implemented under MC No. 10-07-93, requires registration or licensing of all Non-Commercial and Commercial Television Receive Only (TVRO) stations within 60 days.

Registration Requirement and Deadline

All Non-Commercial and Commercial TVRO stations must be registered and/or licensed, respectively, with the Commission's Central Office or Regional Offices within 60 days of this Circular's issuance. Failure to register or license a TVRO station subjects the operator to administrative sanctions.
