Memorandum Order No. 02-02-2017 — IMO Guidance on the Validity of Radiocommunications Equipment Installed and Used on Ships

Status
Active
Issued
February 27, 2017, Quezon City

Relays and implements IMO Maritime Safety Committee (MSC) Circular MSC.1/Circ.1460/Rev.1, addressing possible incompatibility between radiocommunication equipment installed on ships and the revised frequencies and channeling arrangements for maritime HF and VHF bands under Appendices 17 and 18 to the Radio Regulations (2012 Edition).

Background

The MSC approved the original guidance at its ninety-second session (June 2013) on the recommendation of the Sub-Committee on Radiocommunications and Search and Rescue. NTC had separately issued Memorandum Circular No. 08-08-2016 (August 4, 2016) adopting Annex 2, Appendix 17 (Rev. WRC-12) of the Radio Regulations for the maritime mobile service, with implementation effective January 1, 2017. This Order notes that the MSC, at its ninety-seventh session (November 2016), took up a further revision to that guidance.
