Memorandum Circular No. 07-08-2005 — Rules and Regulations on the Allocation and Assignment of 3G Radio Frequency Bands

Status
Active
Issued
2005, pursuant to RA 7925, Act 3846 as amended, and EO 546 series of 1979

Promulgates rules for allocating and assigning radio frequency bands for International Mobile Telecommunications 2000 (IMT2000), also known as Third Generation (3G) mobile telecommunications, in the Philippines.

Section 1 — Allocation of Radio Frequency Bands

Re-allocates the ITU-identified bands 825–845 MHz, 870–890 MHz, 1880–1900 MHz, 1920–1980 MHz, 2110–2170 MHz, and 2010–2025 MHz for IMT2000/3G use, forming part of the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table (NRFAT). These bands are made available for assignment to no more than five qualified public telecommunications entities; the 825–845 MHz and 870–890 MHz bands were, at the time of issuance, already assigned to existing Cellular Mobile Telephone System (CMTS) operators.

Section 2 — Transfer of Affected Users

Addresses the transfer arrangements for existing users of the re-allocated frequency bands as part of the transition to 3G assignment.
