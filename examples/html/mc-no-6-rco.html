MC No. 6 (RCO, 1972) — Submission of End-User Lists and Frequency Compliance Reports

Status
Superseded
Issued
1972-10-12

Issued by the Radio Control Office on October 12, 1972 pursuant to Paragraph 3 of Letter of Instruction No. 13-A, requires franchise holders to submit lists of end-users and reports of compliance with the directive to revert to assigned frequencies, by October 31, 1972.

Required Submissions

Franchise holders must submit, in the prescribed format available at the Radio Control Office and its Field Offices in Cebu City, Tolosa (Leyte), and Davao City: (a) a list of end-users licensed under existing franchise, including station/company names and operator names; and (b) a report of compliance with the directive to revert to assigned frequencies, including their respective end-users.

Enforcement

All radio stations of end-users found not operating on their authorized frequency after October 31, 1972 will be closed, and franchise holders will be held liable under Act 3846 and Act 3396, as amended.
