Memorandum Circular No. 4-09-88 — Prescribed Equipment for Ship Stations of Various Vessels Plying Domestic Routes and Other Requirements

Status
Active
Issued
1988, pursuant to Act 3396, as amended, and Executive Order No. 546 (July 23, 1979)

Ensures that vessels plying domestic Philippine routes carry the necessary radio apparatus to enhance safety of life and property at sea, prohibiting passenger and/or cargo vessels registered with the Philippine government from leaving any Philippine port without the prescribed radio installation.

Prescribed Radio Equipment by Vessel Category

Requirements scale with vessel size and voyage duration. For liner passenger vessels: below 350 gross tons (G.T.) with under 5 hours of navigation need VHF capability on 156.8/156.3/156.6 MHz; 350 G.T. and above with under 5 hours navigation need the same VHF capability; below 350 G.T. with over 5 hours navigation additionally need SSB capability on 2182/4125/6215.5 KHz; and 350 G.T. and above with over 5 hours navigation need a full telegraphy installation (500 KHz), SSB, VHF, and main and reserve receivers with reserve power supply. Separate requirements apply to cargo vessels.
