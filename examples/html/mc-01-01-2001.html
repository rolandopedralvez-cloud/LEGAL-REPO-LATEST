MC 01-01-2001 — Reiteration of Program Standards Amid Destabilization Concerns

Status
Active
Issued
2001-01-17

Reiterates the content restrictions of Memorandum Circular No. 11-12-85 after the Commission received information, through the Office of the Press Secretary, about “suspicious elements” allegedly seeking to create a destabilization move against the government, and adds an explicit prohibition on airing rebellious or terrorist propaganda.

Reiterated Restrictions

Restates paragraphs “5” and “6” of MC 11-12-85 verbatim: stations must provide adequate public service time, conform to the ethics of honest enterprise, avoid obscene/indecent content and false information or content inciting subversive or treasonable acts, and must cut off any broadcast tending to incite treason, rebellion, or sedition, or that is indecent or immoral.

Additional Prohibition

Adds that the airing of rebellious/terrorist propaganda, comments, interviews, information, and similar or related materials shall be prohibited, and requires all broadcast media entities, radio or television, to conform with these guidelines.
