Memorandum Circular No. 04-03-99 — Implementing Guidelines on International Satellite Communications

Status
Active
Issued
1999, pursuant to RA 7925, Act No. 3846 as amended, EO 546 series of 1979, and EO 467 series of 1998

Promulgates implementing guidelines governing access to international satellite communications systems by Philippine public telecommunications entities.

Article I — Access to International Fixed Satellite Systems

Public telecommunications entities enfranchised and certificated to install, operate, and maintain international telecommunications systems and services are allowed direct access to any international fixed satellite system, provided the international satellite operator has an existing reciprocal agreement with the Philippines validated by NTC, the operator has a commercial presence in the country, and Philippine satellite operators are given preference to provide space segment capacity when all factors are equally considered. Entities engaged in research, development, education, health, safety, and rescue are allowed direct access to international satellite systems under separate provision.
