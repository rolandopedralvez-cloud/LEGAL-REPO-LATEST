Memorandum Circular No. 03-03-2005 — Rules and Regulations on Broadcast Messaging Service

Status
Active
Issued
2005, pursuant to RA 7925 and EO 546 series of 1979, to address consumer complaints, welfare, and protection

Regulates Broadcast Messaging Service — sending the same SMS/MMS message to a large number of mobile phones — to protect consumers from unwanted commercial and promotional messaging.

Section 1 — Scope

Applies to all Public Telecom Entities and content providers operating in the Philippines, covering commercial and promotional advertisements and surveys sent via broadcast/push messaging. Does not cover emergency, distress, or public service information messages, network advisories, or consumer welfare and protection advisories.

Section 2 — Definition of Terms

Defines Broadcast Messaging Service as sending the same SMS/MMS message to a large number of mobile phones, Commercial Advertisements as messages soliciting business or generating revenue, and Content Provider as an organization that creates and maintains databases of information from an information provider.
