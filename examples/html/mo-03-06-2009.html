MO 03-06-2009 — Service Performance Standards for Cellular Mobile Telephone Service

Status
Active
Issued
2009-06-30

Pursuant to RA 7925 Sec. 5(a) and 5(e), updates the Grade of Service (GOS) and Drop Call Rate (DCR) standards for Cellular Mobile Telephone Service (CMTS), reflecting the scheduled incremental improvements built into MC No. 07-06-2002 after six years had elapsed.

Updated Standards

Prescribes a Grade of Service of 4% (4 lost calls per 100 call attempts) and a Drop Call Rate of 2% (2 dropped calls per 100 calls), reflecting the cumulative 3% improvement in each metric called for under MC No. 07-06-2002's mandated two-year improvement cycle. Superseded inconsistent prior issuances and took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette or a newspaper of general circulation.
