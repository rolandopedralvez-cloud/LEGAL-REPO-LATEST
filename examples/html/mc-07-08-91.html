MC 7-8-91 — Reiterating PARA Endorsement Requirement for Amateur Radio Club Accreditation

Status
Active
Issued
1991-08-01

Reiterates the policy that an amateur radio club must have an endorsement from the Philippine Amateur Radio Association (PARA) before it can be accredited by the NTC, while confirming that NTC accredits an amateur's right to join any club.

Reiterated Policy

Before an amateur radio club can be accredited by the NTC, an indorsement from the Philippine Amateur Radio Association (PARA) is required; the right of an amateur to be a member of any club remains an NTC-accredited right.
