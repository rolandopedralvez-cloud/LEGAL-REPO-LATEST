Memorandum Order No. 06-06-2017 — Filing of Applications for Renewal of Radio Station Licenses Expiring in the Last Quarter of Each Year

Status
Active
Issued
June 2017, Quezon City

Issued so that NTC can determine, at least 75 days before the end of each year, whether it has exceeded its target revenues, in order to timely request the use of excess income from the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) under the Special Provisions of RA 10924, the General Appropriations Act of 2017, which allows agencies to use excess income to augment current appropriations if obligated by December 31 of that year.

Guidelines

Duly authorized public telecommunications entities (PTEs) must file applications for renewal of radio station licenses (RSLs) expiring in the last quarter (October 1 to December 31) of each year at least 30 days before expiration. All fees, including spectrum user fees (SUF), must be paid upon filing. Renewal applications for stations within a Regional Office's jurisdiction must be filed at that Regional Office. Any inconsistent circular, memorandum, order, or guideline is considered superseded or amended accordingly.
