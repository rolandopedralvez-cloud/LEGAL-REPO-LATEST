Guidelines for the Issuance of Temporary Permit to Operate Ship Station of Newly Acquired Philippine Registered Ships Engaged in International Trade

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2019-12-17
Source/Authority
Act No. 3846, as amended; SOLAS Convention, 1974, as amended

Memorandum Circular No. 06-12-2019 establishes the requirements and procedure for obtaining a Temporary Permit to operate the radio station of a newly acquired Philippine-registered ship engaged in international trade, pending issuance of a regular Ship Station License.

Definitions and general provisions

A Temporary Permit is the Commission's written authority for temporary ship-station operation, issued to a newly acquired Philippine-registered ship located in a foreign port, pending a radio inspection/survey by an NTC-authorized Classification Society whose report becomes the basis for the regular Ship and/or Ship Earth Station License.

Application requirements

Applicants file at the NTC Central Office a letter request (stating delivery date/place, an inventory of installed radio/navigational equipment, and the surveying Classification Society), a copy of the MARINA-issued Approval of Temporary Philippine Registration under the Bareboat Charter Scheme, a Certificate of Inclusion from a Recognized Private Operating Agency or Accounting Authority, valid General Operator's Certificates for two deck officers (excluding the master), and proof of the deck officers' employment contracts.

Validity, fees, and effectivity

A Temporary Permit is valid for three (3) months from issuance unless revoked or cancelled. Applicable fees are a Filing Fee of PHP 180.00, a Temporary Permit Fee of PHP 480.00, and a Documentary Stamp Fee of PHP 30.00. Any inconsistent issuance is deemed superseded or amended, and the Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three true copies with the UP Law Center.
