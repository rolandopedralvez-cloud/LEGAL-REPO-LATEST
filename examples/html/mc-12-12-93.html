MC 12-12-93 — Allocation of Frequency Band 75.225-75.425 MHz for Low Power RF System

Status
Active
Issued
1993-12-03

Pursuant to Act 3846 and existing radio laws, allocates the 75.225-75.425 MHz frequency band for Low Power RF Systems with a limited power of 50 milliwatts or less and F3 (FM) emission.

Allocated Band and Limits

Allocates 75.225-75.425 MHz for Low Power RF Systems limited to 50 milliwatts or less power with F3 (FM) emission. Effective immediately, superseding inconsistent prior circulars.
