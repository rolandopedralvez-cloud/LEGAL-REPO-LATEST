Memorandum Circular No. 4-08-88 — Revised Rules and Regulations Governing Cable Television Systems in the Philippines

Status
Active
Issued
1988, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended, and Executive Order 205

Recognizes the ability of Cable Television Systems / Community Antenna Television (CATV) to expand programming access and improve broadcast signal reach in remote areas. Because public interest requires that monopolies in commercial mass media be regulated or prohibited, the cable TV industry is treated as part of the broadcast media and made subject to NTC's authority to protect the public and promote general welfare.

Section 1 — Purpose

Establishes rules for authorization, licensing, construction, installation, operation, and maintenance of cable television systems; sets technical standards for operators; and provides guidelines for carriage of broadcast signals, program exclusivity, cablecasting, and access channels. A system carrying services under common carrier operations is subject to Certificate of Public Convenience (CPC) requirements after due notice and hearing.

Section 2 — Definitions

Defines core terms including Cable Television System/CATV (excluding facilities serving fewer than 50 subscribers or a single apartment building), Certificate of Authority, Principal Community Contour, Specified Zone (56 km from a television station's licensed reference point), and audience-measurement terms used to determine which broadcast signals a cable system must carry — Substantially Duplicated, Significantly Viewed, Full Network Station, Partial Network Station, Independent Station, Network Programming, Syndicated Program, Series, Off-Network, First-Run Series, Prime Time (6:00–11:00 P.M.), and Cablecasting (programming carried exclusively under the cable operator's own control).

Section 3 — Certificate of Authority

No cable television system may be constructed, installed, operated, or maintained without a Certificate of Authority granted by NTC, which specifies the permits and licenses required to be secured after issuance.

Section 4 — Licenses and Permits

A cable TV head-end station must separately obtain a permit to purchase and/or possess locally acquired or imported equipment, among other required licenses from the Commission.

Section 5 — Technical Requirements

Plans and designs for a CATV system submitted to the Commission must be signed and sealed by a duly registered Electronics and Communications Engineer (ECE).
