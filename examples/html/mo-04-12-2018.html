Memorandum Order No. 04-12-2018 — Uninterrupted Telecommunications Services During the Holiday Season

Status
Superseded by events — issued for the 2018 Christmas and New Year season specifically, though the underlying policy is reissued each year
Issued
December 2018, Quezon City

Issued pursuant to Republic Act No. 7925, Executive Order No. 546 series of 1979, and Commonwealth Act No. 146 (the Public Service Act, as amended), directing all public telecommunication entities to ensure uninterrupted telecommunications services during the Christmas and New Year season.

Directives to Public Telecommunication Entities

All service offerings, including internet/broadband access, must not exclude December 25 and January 1. Entities must undertake all necessary network adjustments and maintenance to accommodate the expected increase in traffic during the season, and deploy sufficient operations and maintenance personnel to respond to service issues.
