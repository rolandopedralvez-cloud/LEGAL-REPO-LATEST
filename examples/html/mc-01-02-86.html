Memorandum Circular No. 01-02-86 — Guidelines in the Conduct of Restricted Land Mobile Radiotelephone Operator Seminar

Status
Amended — later supplemented by MC 09-07-92
Issued
1986

Under MC No. 70885 (approved September 16, 1985), accredited radio training schools may conduct seminars for Restricted Radiotelephone Operator's Certificate applicants for land mobile radiotelephone stations, in lieu of the radio operator examination prescribed by Department Order No. 6, Series of 1949. Pending sufficient accredited training schools, NTC conducts the seminar itself under these guidelines.

Request for Seminar

A written request to NTC must include a construction permit, radio station license, or other authorization showing the assigned land mobile frequency; a list of applicants certified as regular employees or members of the sponsoring organization and qualified per MC 70885; a proposed seminar date and place; and a P10.00 filing fee per applicant.
