Memorandum Circular No. 1-04-88 — Rules and Regulations Governing Equipment Provided by Customers/Subscribers of Public Networks

Status
Amended — later revised by MC 02-01-2001
Issued
1988, pursuant to the Public Service Act as amended and DOTC Circular 87-188

Establishes the original rules governing Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) — equipment located on a customer's or subscriber's premises for connection to public telecommunications network facilities, limited to equipment enumerated in the Circular's Table A.

Section 1 — Purpose

Defines conditions and administrative procedures for CPE type approval; conditions, rules, and regulations for accrediting CPE suppliers; the responsibilities of CPE suppliers; conditions under which customer-supplied CPE may be connected to a public telecommunications network; and the responsibilities of the network operator regarding connected customer-supplied CPE.

Section 2 — Type Approval

Type approval evaluates CPE for compatibility with the public telecommunications network, ensuring a certificate of type approval is granted only to equipment that, when connected, will not cause harm or compromise the network's safety.
