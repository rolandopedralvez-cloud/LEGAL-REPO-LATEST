Memorandum Circular No. 10-18-90 — Rules and Regulations Governing Public Repeater Network Services in the Philippines

Status
Active
Issued
1990, pursuant to Act No. 3846 as amended, Act No. 146 (Public Service Act) as amended, and EO 546 (July 23, 1979)

Establishes rules for the authorization, installation, operation, and maintenance of Public Repeater Networks (PRNS) in the Philippines, and sets the technical requirements for their operation and maintenance.

Definition of Terms

A Public Repeater Network is a public radio communications system offering radio repeater services to the general public at Commission-authorized rates. Shared Repeater Service allows a group of users to share a common communication path, while Trunked Repeater Service allows a large number of subscribers to share a group of communication paths. A Repeater Station is a fixed radio station that automatically retransmits signals received from one or more mobile stations on one frequency to mobile stations on another frequency, or vice versa.
