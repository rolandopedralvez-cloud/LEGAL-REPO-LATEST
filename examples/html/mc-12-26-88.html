Memorandum Circular No. 12-26-88 — Guidelines and Procedures for the Grant of Authorization Under the Government Personal Radio Service to Certain Members of the Executive, Legislative and Judicial Branches of the Government

Status
Active
Issued
1988

Rationalizes the grant of authorizations for high government officials in the Executive, Legislative, and Judicial branches to acquire and operate radiocommunications equipment for official communications needs.

Definition of Terms

A Special Radio Authorization is a written authority issued by NTC to government officials for operating their personal radio communication system. The Government Personal Radio Service is a radio communication system in the fixed and land mobile services reserved for use by high government officials in recognition of the nature of their public functions.

Government Positions Covered

In the Executive Branch: the Vice-President, Secretaries and Undersecretaries, and Commissioners and Associate Deputy Commissioners. Similar covered positions are enumerated for the Legislative and Judicial branches.
