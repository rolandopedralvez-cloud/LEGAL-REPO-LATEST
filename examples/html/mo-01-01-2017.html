Memorandum Order No. 01-01-2017 — Guidelines for Granting General Amnesty for Unregistered Radio Equipment

Status
Active (one-time registration window; guidance value continues)
Issued
January 9, 2017, Quezon City

Issued in the interest of the service and pursuant to NTC's thrust to abate the proliferation of unregistered radio equipment, offering owners, possessors, and users of such equipment an opportunity to comply with existing rules and regulations by registering undocumented radio equipment.

Coverage and Registration Period

Applies only to (a) Land Mobile Radio Service for Civic Action Groups, government agencies, and private networks (excluding telecommunications and broadcast entities), and (b) Amateur Radio Service. The registration period ran from February 1–28, 2017, and subsequent licensing of registered equipment follows existing rules and the usual licensing procedures.

Registration Fees and Procedure

Fees were set at Php 300.00 for portable VHF/UHF equipment, Php 500.00 for mobile/base VHF/UHF equipment, and Php 500.00 for HF radio equipment. Registration was accepted only at NTC Regional and District Offices or during Mobile Licensing Activities. Registration does not by itself authorize construction or operation of the equipment without a corresponding permit or license; unauthorized use, including use by persons other than the registrant, remains grounds for administrative sanctions, fines, or penalties. Registered equipment remains at the accountability of the registrant, and licenses already revoked by final Commission decision are excluded from the amnesty.
