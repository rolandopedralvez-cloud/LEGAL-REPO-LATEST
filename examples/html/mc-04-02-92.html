Memorandum Circular No. 4-2-92 — Amendments to the 1974 Safety of Life at Sea Convention Concerning Radiocommunications for the Global Maritime Distress and Safety System

Status
Active — amends MC No. 02-2-82
Issued
1992

Sets out guidelines and procedures, to be strictly observed by all Philippine-registered vessels engaged in international trade, implementing amendments to the 1974 Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS) Convention concerning radiocommunications for the Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS).

Definitions

Defines the Cargo Ship Safety Radio Certificate, issued after inspection to a cargo vessel with radio installation complying with SOLAS requirements; the Exemption Certificate, for cargo vessels on voyages where distance from shore, voyage length, absence of navigational hazards, and other safety conditions make full application of the regulations unreasonable; Classification Society, an international body that inspects ships and offshore structures to safeguard life and property at sea; Ship Radio Station License, NTC's authorization for operating a mobile maritime station aboard a vessel; and Ship Earth Station License, the corresponding authorization for shipboard satellite communications equipment.
