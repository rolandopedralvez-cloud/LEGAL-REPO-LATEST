MC 04-05-2003 — Non-Posting of Certificate of Registration for PTRS Subscriber Radio Units

Status
Active
Issued
2003-05-01

Provides that posting of certificates of registration on the radio units of subscribers of duly enfranchised and authorized Public Trunked Radio System (PTRS) operators shall not be required, subject to conditions, pursuant to the Public Telecommunications Policy Act of 1995 (RA 7925) and the Radio Control Law (Act 3846, as amended).

Conditions for Non-Posting

The PTRS must ensure that subscriber radio units covered by certificates of registration are properly identified and that such identifications are not removed, and must submit to the Commission an updated list of subscribers not later than ten (10) days after the end of each quarter.

Enforcement

Subscriber radio units not covered by PTRS identification are subject to apprehension and appropriate penalties, and the Commission shall prosecute any person or entity using fake, tampered, or unauthorized PTRS identification. The Order took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified copies with the UP Law Center.
