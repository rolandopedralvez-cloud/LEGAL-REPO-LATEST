Memorandum Circular No. 10-10-97 — Spectrum User Fees

Status
Active
Issued
1997, pursuant to RA 7925 (Public Telecommunications Policy Act) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations

Establishes annual Spectrum User Fees (SUF), collected in addition to all existing fees NTC charges, based on the amount of spectrum used, the type of service offered, and the economic classification of the areas served.

Payment Deadline and Penalties

SUF must be paid by January 31 each year, with a 25% surcharge on the amount due plus 1% of the principal per month of delay for late payment. A carrier defaulting on SUF payment for one year faces Commission action and, after due process and a finding of guilt, immediate transfer of its frequencies to other duly authorized carriers providing the same service, without adversely affecting the defaulting carrier's subscribers.

Fee Schedule

For Cellular Mobile Telephone Service (CMTS) in the 800 MHz and 900 MHz bands, fees are PHP 10.00/KHz/station in Metro Manila, PHP 5.00/KHz/station in Highly Urbanized Cities, and PHP 2.50/KHz/station in all other areas, with additional schedules for other services.
