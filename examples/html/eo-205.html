Executive Order No. 205, s. 1987 — Regulating the Operation of Cable Antenna Television (CATV) Systems in the Philippines, and for Other Purposes

Status
Amended — later supplemented by EO 436's policy guidelines
Effective Date
June 30, 1987
Signed By
President Corazon C. Aquino, by Executive Secretary Joker P. Arroyo

Sections 1–2 — Certificate of Authority

CATV system operation is open to Philippine citizens, or corporations/cooperatives/ associations wholly owned and managed by such citizens, under a Certificate of Authority granted by the NTC on a non-exclusive basis for up to 15 years, renewable, provided the authority does not infringe on television and broadcast markets.

Sections 3–4 — Eminent Domain and Emergency Powers

Grantees are authorized to exercise eminent domain for efficient system maintenance and operation. The President reserves the right, in war, rebellion, public peril, or national emergency, to close a grantee's system or authorize government use/possession without compensation.

Sections 5–8 — Taxation, Implementation, Repeal, Effectivity

Grantees pay regular income tax plus a franchise tax of 3% of gross receipts from business under the Certificate of Authority. NTC is authorized to issue implementing rules. Presidential Decree No. 1512 (June 11, 1978) and inconsistent issuances are repealed or modified. Effective immediately, Manila, June 30, 1987.
