MC 09-12-95 — Recognition of the Radio Telecom Dealers Association of the Philippines (RTDAP)

Status
Active
Issued
1995-12-07

Pursuant to Act 3846 and MC No. 02-05-88, recognizes the Radio Telecom Dealers Association of the Philippines (RTDAP) as the national association representing radio dealers, service centers, and manufacturers in dealings with the Commission.

Recognition

Formally recognizes RTDAP as the national organization for radio dealers, service centers, and manufacturers, aimed at fostering cordial relationships and efficient coordination between the industry and government.
