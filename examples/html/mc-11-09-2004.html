MC 11-09-2004 — Mandatory Taping of Radio and Television Programs

Status
Active
Issued
2004-09-24

Pursuant to Act 3846 and EO 546, directs radio and television stations to tape specified categories of programs, retain the recordings for at least ten days, and make them available to the Commission upon request, as part of NTC's supervisory function over broadcast content.

Covered Programs and Announcement Requirement

Covers talk shows/live phone-in programs, live interviews/public affairs/public service programs, news analysis/editorials/commentaries/documentaries, and magazine/counseling programs (with named contemporary examples). Stations must air an announcement after each such broadcast informing viewers where to direct comments or objections, to be received within five days of broadcast.

Penalties

Failure to comply warrants a P5,000 fine for a first offense, and fine and/or suspension/revocation of licenses and permits for subsequent offenses. Took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified copies with the UP Law Center.
