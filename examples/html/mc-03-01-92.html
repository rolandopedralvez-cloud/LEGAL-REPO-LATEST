MC 03-01-92 — Guidelines for Commercial Radiotelegraph/Radiotelephone Operator Exams for IETC/BSIE Graduates

Status
Active
Issued
1992-01-23

Pursuant to a 1990 Memorandum of Agreement between DECS/BTVE and DOTC/NTC, sets guidelines for accepting and processing commercial radiotelegraph/radiotelephone operator examination applications from graduates of the Industrial Electronics Technician Course (IETC) and Bachelor of Science in Industrial Education (Electronics Technology).

Transitional Acceptance and Prospective Restriction

Applications from IETC/BSIE graduates with a DECS special order issued before this Circular's approval are accepted and processed, and passing graduates receive radio operator certificates upon application and fee payment; previously issued certificates to such graduates remain valid and renewable. From this Circular's effectivity forward, however, the Commission will only accept examination applications from graduates of the General Radio Communications Operator Course (GROC), Communications Technician Course, or Broadcast Technician Course — not IETC or BSIE.
