Memorandum Circular No. 09-07-96 — Amending Memorandum Circular No. 11-21-88

Status
Active — amends MC No. 11-21-88
Issued
1996, pursuant to Act No. 3846, as amended, to clarify the vague definition of intended users of the Personal Radio Service (PRS)

Amends the implementing rules and regulations of the Personal Radio Service (PRS) under MC No. 11-21-88, clarifying who qualifies for the service to improve applicant screening.

New Definitions Added to Section 2 of MC 11-21-88

Qualified Persons are professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, accountants, etc.) maintaining a shop, clinic, or office with five or fewer staff and without adequate telecommunications facilities. Small Business Entities are businesses with at least five organic personnel, paid-up capital not exceeding P500,000, located in an area with inadequate telecommunications facilities.
