Memorandum Circular No. 8-7-98 — Amending Memorandum Circular No. 13-11-91, Guidelines in the Issuance of the General Operator's Certificate (GOC)

Status
Active — amends MC 13-11-91
Issued
1998, in compliance with ITU International Radio Regulations and the STCW Convention's 1996 Conference Resolution, Chapter IV/1 and IV/2

Adds mandatory minimum requirements for certification of GMDSS radio personnel to the existing General Operator's Certificate (GOC) issuance guidelines.

Additional Requirements

A GOC applicant must additionally demonstrate knowledge of search and rescue radiocommunications (including IMO Merchant Ship Search and Rescue Manual procedures), means to prevent and mitigate false distress alerts, ship reporting systems, radio medical services, the International Code of Signals and Standard Marine Communication Phrases, and English proficiency for safety-of-life-at-sea communications. A licensed deck officer must also complete radiotelephony training from an NTC-accredited radio training school, and applicants must demonstrate competence providing radio services during emergencies.
