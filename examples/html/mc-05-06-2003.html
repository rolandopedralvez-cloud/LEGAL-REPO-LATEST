MC 05-06-2003 — Amendment to MC 4-3-99 on International Satellite Communications

Status
Active
Issued
2003-06-10

Pursuant to RA 7925, Act 3846, and EO No. 467 (International Satellite Communications Policy), amends Section 1 of MC No. 4-3-99 to allow accredited UN bodies and international governmental organizations direct access to international satellite systems under specified conditions.

Conditions for Direct Access

UN bodies and accredited international governmental organizations may access international fixed/mobile satellite systems directly if: the equipment is used solely to access that body's own global network; the body does not provide commercial satellite access to the public; the service is not already provided by an authorized Philippine PTE; Philippine satellite operators cannot meet the space segment capacity requirement; and the Department of Foreign Affairs attests to and endorses the organization's application.
