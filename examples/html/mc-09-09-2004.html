Memorandum Circular No. 09-09-2004 — Guidelines for the Implementation of SOLAS Convention Amendment Requiring Automatic Identification System (AIS) and International Ship and Port Facility Security Code (ISPS)

Status
Active
Issued
2004, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended, EO 546, and the SOLAS Convention 1974, as amended

Implements SOLAS Chapter V/Regulation 19 (carriage requirements for shipborne navigational systems and Automatic Identification System equipment) and Chapter XI-2/Regulation 6 (provision of a Ship Security Alert System), to enhance maritime safety and security aboard Philippine-registered ships.

Objectives and Coverage

Provides specific implementation guidelines for the AIS carriage requirement and Ship Security Alert System provisions. Covers all Philippine-registered ships engaged in international voyages, as well as Philippine-registered ships primarily documented for domestic trade but temporarily allowed by the Administration, in coordination with the Commission, to undertake international voyages.
