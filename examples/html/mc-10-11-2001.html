MC 10-11-2001 — Extension of the FM Broadcast Band

Status
Active
Issued
2001-11-14

Pursuant to Rule 600 of MC 8-9-95 (IRR of RA 7925), and after technical evaluation and a public hearing with no opposition registered, extends the FM broadcast band to 87.5-108.0 MHz.

Extended Band and Conditions

Extends the FM broadcast band to 87.5-108.0 MHz, subject to conditions: assigned extended-band channels must be in areas where TV channel 6 (82.0-88.0 MHz) is not in use; assignees must strictly comply with appropriate protection ratios; and operations must always be strictly on a Non-Interference Basis (NIB).

Effect

Superseded any inconsistent circular, order, or memorandum, and took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three copies with the UP Law Center.
