MC 06-89 — Guidelines to Correct Irregular Practices in Radio Training Schools

Status
Active
Issued
1989-06-29

Pursuant to Act 3846, promulgates remedial measures to correct irregular practices among Radio Training Schools, addressing both schools with expired radio station licenses and schools operating without NTC accreditation or registration.

Schools with Expired Licenses

Radio Training Schools with expired permits/licenses are given six months from this Circular's approval to renew their Radio Station License if they intend to continue operating; graduates of schools that fail to renew are barred from taking NTC's commercial radiotelephone/radiotelegraph operator examinations.

Unrecognized/Unregistered Schools

Schools recognized by DECS/BTVE but not accredited by NTC must secure the required NTC permits/licenses before the end of the first semester of school year 1989, or their graduates cannot sit for NTC examinations; graduates of such schools may test only after the school secures accreditation and the graduates complete a 133-hour Radio Station Practice completion course. Schools are warned that repeat violations will be dealt with more severely.
