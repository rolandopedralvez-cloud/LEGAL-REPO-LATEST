Memorandum Circular No. 03-01-95 — Sale, Importation and Use of Amateur Radio Equipment

Status
Active
Issued
1995, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended, and Executive Order 546

Issued in view of the indiscriminate use by some persons of amateur equipment for services other than the amateur service or outside their assigned frequency bands, to establish guidelines for the sale, importation, and use of amateur radio equipment.

Definition of Terms

Amateur Radio Equipment is radio communications equipment intended for use in the amateur service for self-training, intercommunication, and technical experimentation by legitimate amateurs. Expandable Amateur equipment has an operating frequency channel/range that can be programmed, tuned, or modified from its original specifications; Non-Expandable equipment cannot be so modified.
