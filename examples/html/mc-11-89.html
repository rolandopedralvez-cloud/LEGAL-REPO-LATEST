MC 11-89 — Allowing ECE Graduates to Take the Commercial Radiotelephone Operator's Examination

Status
Active
Issued
1989-01-01

Allows graduates of the Bachelor of Science in Electronics & Communications Engineering (ECE) course, who must hold a Commercial Radiotelephone Operator's Certificate to operate radio stations under Act 3846 and Department Order No. 5, to take the Commission's regular commercial radiotelephone operator's examination, subject to specified conditions.

Eligibility Conditions

An ECE graduate applicant must have a valid Special Order number from DECS, must have undergone training/seminar on radio laws and regulations, and must obtain certification from their employer that they are performing radio operator duties.
