MC No. 5 (RCO, 1972) — Mandatory Registration of Unregistered Radio Transmitters

Status
Superseded
Issued
1972-10-12

Issued by the Radio Control Office on October 12, 1972 pursuant to Letter of Instruction No. 13-A of the President, requires all persons, companies, and corporations (other than authorized radio dealers and manufacturers) possessing unregistered radio transmitters or transceivers to register them without penalty by November 15, 1972.

Registration Requirement

All persons, companies, and corporations other than authorized radio dealers and manufacturers possessing unregistered radio transmitters and/or transceivers, irrespective of source or origin, must register them with the Radio Control Office in Quezon City or its Field Offices in Cebu City, Tolosa (Leyte), and Davao City, not later than November 15, 1972, without penalty.

Consequence of Non-Compliance

Failure to comply within the announced deadline subjects those concerned to the provisions of Act 3846, as amended, and to confiscation of the unregistered radio transmitters and/or transceivers.
