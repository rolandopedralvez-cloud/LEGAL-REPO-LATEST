MC 07-06-96 — Implementing Guidelines of MC 04-03-96 (Spread Spectrum Technology)

Status
Active
Issued
1996-06-17

Implements Memorandum Circular No. 04-03-96 concerning spread spectrum technology, setting licensing guidelines for radio equipment using this technology, including frequency band limits, power caps, and technician requirements.

Frequency and Power Limits

Authorization for sale and operation of spread spectrum equipment is limited to the 915-925 MHz, 2400-2483.5 MHz, and 5725-5850.0 MHz bands, with a maximum of one watt effective radiated power (ERP). Equipment must be type-approved or type-accepted by the Equipment and Standards Division prior to licensing, and operation is on a secondary, non-priority, non-interference basis.

Technician Requirements and Enforcement

The spread spectrum network must be operated and maintained by at least one radio technician holding a valid first-class commercial radiotelephone operator certificate, under the supervision of a licensed Electronics and Communications Engineer, except cordless telephones which follow MC No. 07-07-86. The Commission may cancel, suspend, or revoke a license for equipment causing harmful interference until the interference is corrected.
