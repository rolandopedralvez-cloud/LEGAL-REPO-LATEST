MC 6-7-97 — Further Amending MC 9-4-94A on Amateur Radio License Renewal Proof

Status
Active
Issued
1997-07-30

Further amends Memorandum Circular No. 9-4-94A to strengthen implementation of the Amateur Radio Regulations, requiring that proof of involvement in amateur radio activities (for license renewal) and proof of orientation seminar attendance (for Class C/D exams) be endorsed by an NTC-accredited Amateur Radio Club.

Amended Endorsement Requirement

Requires that the proof of involvement in amateur radio activities before Amateur License renewal, and the proof of orientation seminar attendance before taking the amateur Class "C" and/or "D" examination, be endorsed by an NTC-accredited Amateur Radio Club. Took effect upon approval, superseding inconsistent prior issuances.
