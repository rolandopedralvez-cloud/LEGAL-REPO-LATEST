Memorandum Circular No. 07-08-2004 — Rules and Regulations Governing the Repair, Servicing and Maintenance of Mobile Phones

Status
Active
Issued
2004, pursuant to Act No. 3846, as amended, EO 546, and RA 7925

Enables NTC to monitor and control the repair, servicing, and maintenance of mobile phone units, parts, and accessories in the Philippines, in the public interest.

Definition of Terms

Defines Mobile Phone as a handheld radio communications device using cellular mobile telephone system (CMTS) technology to transmit and receive voice, data, and multimedia services by radio waves; Mobile Phone Service Center (MPSC), an entity engaged in repair, servicing, or maintenance of mobile phone units and accessories; the Mobile Phone Service Center Permit (MPSCP), NTC's authorization to operate such a center; and Unauthorized Servicing, defined as any service that alters a mobile phone's International Mobile Equipment Identity (IMEI) number or code.
