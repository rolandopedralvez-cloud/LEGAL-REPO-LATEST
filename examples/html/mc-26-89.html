MC 26-89 — Modification of Frequency Allocation for Aeronautical Mobile (R) Service in 136-137 MHz

Status
Active
Issued
1989-01-01

Pursuant to Act No. 3846 as amended, Executive Order No. 546, and ITU Radio Regulations, implements ITU Recommendation No. 404 to protect the Aeronautical Mobile (R) Service's use of the 136-137 MHz band for air navigation safety.

Protection Requirements

All Fixed Service and Mobile Service stations (except Aeronautical Mobile (R) Service) and Space Operation Service (Space-to-Earth) stations operating in the 136-137 MHz band must, after January 1, 1990, take all possible steps to protect the Aeronautical Mobile (R) Service.

Secondary Status and Transfer

Fixed and Mobile Service stations (except Aeronautical Mobile (R)) holding secondary allocation status in the band must cease operation once Aeronautical Mobile (R) Service stations begin operating there, and must transfer to appropriate VHF or UHF bands on or before January 1, 1990. Effective immediately, superseding contrary circulars.
