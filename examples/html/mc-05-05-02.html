Memorandum Circular No. 05-05-02 — Rules and Regulations on the Provision of High Speed Networks and Connectivity to IT Hub Areas

Status
Active
Issued
2002, pursuant to RA 7925, EO 546 series of 1979, and RA 8792 (the E-Commerce Law)

Issued consistent with the government's goal of developing a viable, efficient, reliable, and universal telecommunications infrastructure using the best available and affordable technologies, in order to stimulate growth and development of information and communications technology (ICT).

Definition of Terms

Defines Information Technology as matters concerned with furthering computer science and technology and the design, development, installation, and implementation of information systems and applications; High Speed Networks as networks with a capacity of at least 2.048 Mbps; and IT Hub Areas as specific areas declared by the Board of Investments and the Department of Trade and Industry (BOI-DTI) where industries requiring high speed networks, services, and connectivity are located.
