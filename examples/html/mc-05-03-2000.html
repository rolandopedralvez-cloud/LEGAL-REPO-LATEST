MC 05-03-2000 — Guidelines on Annual Testing of 406 MHz Satellite EPIRB

Status
Active
Issued
2000-03-01

Pursuant to Act 3846 and Act 3396, and following the 70th session of the IMO Maritime Safety Committee addressing SOLAS regulation IV/15.9, promulgates guidelines for the annual testing of 406 MHz satellite Emergency Position Indicating Radio Beacons (EPIRBs) aboard Philippine-registered vessels.

Testing Procedure

Testing must use suitable test equipment and include: checking mounting for float-free operation; visual inspection for defects; the self-test routine; verifying the EPIRB ID marking and decoding the transmitted identity signal; checking battery and (if applicable) hydrostatic release expiry dates; checking the 406 MHz signal frequency without triggering a distress transmission; checking the 121.5 MHz homing signal if possible; confirming maintenance by an approved servicing station; and remounting the EPIRB, verifying no transmission was started.

Reporting

A written report must be submitted to the NTC Radio Regulation and Licensing Department within ten days of each annual test. Effective immediately, superseding inconsistent prior circulars, orders, or instructions.
