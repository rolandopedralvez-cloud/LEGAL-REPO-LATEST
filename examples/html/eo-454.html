Executive Order No. 454 — Transferring Back the National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) Under the Supervision and Control of the Department of Transportation and Communications (DOTC)

Status
Amended — NTC's placement was subsequently changed again by EO 648 (2007)
Effective Date
August 16, 2005
Signed By
President Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo, by Executive Secretary Eduardo R. Ermita

Recites that EO 546 (1979) originally created NTC under the supervision and control of the Ministry (now Department) of Transportation and Communications, and that EO 269 (2004) later placed NTC under the direct supervision and control of the Commission on Information and Communications Technology (CICT). Finding that transferring NTC back to DOTC will streamline bureaucratic operations, and invoking the President's continuing reorganization authority under the Administrative Code of 1987, this Order transfers NTC back under the supervision and control of DOTC. Section 1 separately places the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office under the Department of Health. Inconsistent issuances are repealed or modified. Effective immediately.
