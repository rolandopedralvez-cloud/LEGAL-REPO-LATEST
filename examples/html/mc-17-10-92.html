MC 17-10-92 — Issuance of Demonstration/Propagation Test Permit

Status
Active
Issued
1992-10-22

Pursuant to Act No. 3846 as amended, authorizes issuance of a Demonstration/Propagation Test Permit to allow public and private network users to evaluate radio equipment and determine the technical capability or performance of radio systems, equipment, or path links.

Designated Test Frequencies

Lists designated frequency pairs for testing, including 5467.50/10187.50 KHz, 38.375/39.380 MHz, 142.800/148.930 MHz, 163.950/225.850 MHz, 484.000/497.900 MHz, 860.000/904.00 MHz, and 1810.00/2120.00 MHz, plus other frequencies the Commission may authorize.

Permit Terms

The permit is issued on a non-exclusive, non-interference basis upon request of a duly authorized representative, valid for a period not exceeding thirty (30) days and automatically recalled thereafter; no test may be conducted without first obtaining the permit. Effective October 22, 1992, superseding inconsistent prior circulars.
