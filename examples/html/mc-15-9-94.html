MC 15-9-94 — Procedures for Increasing Cable Television Channel Capacity

Status
Active
Issued
1994-08-30

Sets simplified procedures for cable television operators applying to increase the number of channels offered, implementing Section 6.3.3 of Memorandum Circular No. 4-08-88, in line with the Government's policy on decentralization and liberalization.

Application Procedure

An applicant for increased operating channels need not file a motion, as the application will not undergo quasi-judicial process; instead, the applicant applies with the Commission or a Regional Office through a letter indicating the number of additional channels, existing channels, program sources, and mode of transmission (off-air or satellite feed).

Satellite-Fed Channels and Program Disclosure

If additional channels are received via satellite, the operator's existing and additional TVRO system must comply with MC 10-07-93. Applicants must also submit the format, title, and schedule of programs to be cablecast on the additional channels.
