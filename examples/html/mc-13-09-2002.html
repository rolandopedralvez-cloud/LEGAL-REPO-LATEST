MC 13-09-2002 — Amending Mandatory NAVTEX/EPIRB Rules for Coastwise Vessels

Status
Active
Issued
2002-09-27

Amends Sections II (Scope/Coverage) and IV (Date of Mandatory Compliance) of Memorandum Circular No. 01-01-2002, following sub-committee meetings, revising which coastwise vessels must carry NAVTEX Receivers and Satellite EPIRBs and their compliance deadlines.

Revised Coverage

Covered Philippine vessels engaged in coastwise trade are: passenger and general cargo vessels of 150 G.T. and above; tankers and high-speed craft; fishing vessels of 250 G.T. and above; and tugboats and barges operating coastwise.

Revised Compliance Dates

Installation of the Satellite EPIRB is required on or before October 31, 2002, and installation of the NAVTEX Receiver on or before December 31, 2002. The Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified copies with the UP Law Center.
