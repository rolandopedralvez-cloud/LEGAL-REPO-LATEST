Memorandum Circular No. 15-10-98 — Guidelines in the Accreditation of SRRS Sales Outlet

Status
Active — implements the Short-Range Radio Service licensing framework under MC 01-01-98 and MC 07-06-98
Issued
1998

Requires any person, entity, corporation, association, or agency intending to stock and/or sell Short-Range Radio Service (SRRS) equipment to be duly accredited by NTC.

Accreditation Requirements

Minimum requirements include paid-up capitalization of at least P250,000, a valid distributorship or representation agreement with an accredited SRRS equipment manufacturer/dealer, proof that the equipment sold is duly type approved, a valid service or maintenance agreement with an NTC-accredited dealer/manufacturer, and a valid mayor's permit. Applicants must submit a letter of intent, SEC or Bureau of Domestic Trade registration papers, proof of paid-up capitalization, and the agreements and permits described above.
