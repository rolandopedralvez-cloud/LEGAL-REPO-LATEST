MC 08-11-2005 — Reiterating Strict Compliance with EO 255 on Original Pilipino Music

Status
Active
Issued
2005-11-09

Pursuant to Presidential Memorandum Order No. 193 (October 4, 2005), reiterates strict compliance with Executive Order No. 255 (July 25, 1987), mandating the playing of original Pilipino musical compositions on radio stations with musical-format programming.

Mandate

All radio stations are mandated to play or broadcast a minimum of four (4) original Pilipino musical compositions in every clock-hour of a program with a musical format, in accordance with Section 1 of Executive Order No. 255. Issued for immediate compliance.
