Memorandum Circulars

MC 11-08-96 — Suspension of Applications for New Radio Service Center, Dealer, and Manufacturer

Status
Superseded
Issued
1996-08-15

Temporarily suspends new applications for radio service centers, dealers, and manufacturers to allow the Commission to review existing categorization rules under MC 02-05-88 and address unauthorized equipment importation, illegal sales, and fly-by-night dealers.

Reasons for Suspension

Cites the need to review the categorization of Radio Service Centers, Dealers, and Manufacturers under Section 4 of MC 02-05-88; continued entry of non-type-accepted and unauthorized equipment imports; illegal sale, use, and operation of non-type-accepted and unregistered equipment; and proliferation of fly-by-night radio dealers.

Duration and Oversight

The Radio Regulations and Licensing Department (RRLD) monitors implementation directly or through Regional Offices. The suspension is automatically lifted upon issuance of remedial measures or after six months from effectivity, whichever comes first.
