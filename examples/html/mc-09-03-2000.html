MC 09-03-2000 — Radio Frequency Allocation for Cellular Mobile Telephone Service

Status
Active
Issued
2000-03-09

Pursuant to RA 7925, EO 109 (1993), and EO 546 (1979), reallocates specified frequency bands for Cellular Mobile Telephone Service (CMTS), citing declining international accounting rates, uneven local-exchange buildout, industry consolidation, and network congestion among existing operators.

Reallocated Bands and Eligibility

Reallocates 1745-1750/1840-1845 MHz, 1750-1755/1845-1850 MHz, 1765-1770/1860-1865 MHz, and 1770-1775/1865-1870 MHz for assignment to existing CMTS operators and PTEs authorized to install CMTS networks. Only PTEs that have satisfactorily complied with local-exchange-line obligations under EO 109 and RA 7925 and have no existing CMTS authorization are eligible for new grants; existing operators may receive additional frequencies if they can prove congestion attributable solely to insufficient frequency.

Transfer and Effectivity

Transfer of previously authorized stations within these bands is governed by Rule 603 of MC 3-3-96. The reallocation took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified copies with the UP Law Center.
