Allocation of the Frequency Band, 5351.5-5366.5 KHz, For Amateur Radio Service on Secondary Basis

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2017-06-21
Source/Authority
Act No. 3846, as amended; RA 7925; EO 546, s. 1979

Memorandum Circular No. 04-06-2017 allocates the 5351.5-5366.5 kHz frequency band to the Amateur Radio Service on a secondary basis, consistent with the periodic review of the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table (NRFAT) and applicable ITU frequency allocations.

Background

The Circular cites Rule 600 of MC 8-9-95 (implementing RA 7925), which subjects spectrum allocation to periodic review, and Rule 601 of MC 3-3-96, which requires the NRFAT to be reviewed every two years and kept consistent with the International Table of Radio Frequency Allocations issued by the ITU. It notes that such reviews are conducted in consultation with the industry and affected parties.

Allocation

Pursuant to RA 7925, EO 546 series of 1979, and Act No. 3846, as amended, and in accordance with ITU frequency allocations, the NTC allocates the frequency band 5351.5-5366.5 kHz for Amateur Radio Service on a secondary basis, subject to national and international regulations including ITU Radio Regulations Footnote No. 5.133B. The band forms part of the NRFAT. Any inconsistent circular, order, or memorandum is deemed superseded.

Effectivity

The Circular takes effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and the filing of three copies with the UP Law Center.
