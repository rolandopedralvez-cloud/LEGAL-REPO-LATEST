MC 11-09-2002 — Minimum Number of Stations for Conventional Radio Communications Networks

Status
Active
Issued
2002-09-27

Imposes minimum numbers of radio stations for new applications for authority to establish, operate, and maintain a conventional non-carrier radio communications network, to conserve and effectively manage the radio frequency spectrum, pursuant to Act 3846 and in furtherance of MC 10-08-91.

Duplex Mode Networks

Applications for a new Conventional Radio Communications Network on Duplex Mode must have at least twenty-five (25) radio stations per repeater.

Simplex Mode Networks and Exceptions

Applications for a new network on Simplex Mode must have at least six (6) radio stations. Networks catering to national security, public safety, or overcoming man-made or natural barriers are evaluated case by case. Issuance of radio station licenses must at all times comply with these minimums.
