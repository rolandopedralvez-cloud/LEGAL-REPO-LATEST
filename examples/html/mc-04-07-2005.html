MC 04-07-2005 — Extension of the UHF TV Broadcast Band

Status
Active
Issued
2005-01-01

Pursuant to Rule 600 of MC 8-9-95 and EO 546, extends the UHF TV broadcast band by designating the 674-698 MHz band for UHF TV broadcast use, following review and rationalization of the broadcast spectrum to keep pace with technology developments.

Extended Band

Designates the 674-698 MHz band for UHF TV broadcast service use, subject to Rule 603 of MC 3-3-96 governing transfer of previously authorized stations within the band. Superseded inconsistent circulars, orders, or memoranda, and took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
