MC 05-02-81 — Re-Extending Deadline for VHF Equipment on Vessels

Status
Superseded
Issued
1981-02-13

Re-extends, for the last time as stated, the deadline under MC 08-10-80 for all ships and vessels with radio stations to be equipped with VHF transceiver equipment, in response to continued representations from shipping and fishing operators for more time to comply.

Background

Memorandum Circular No. 05-07-80 (July 18, 1980) required all ships/vessels with radio stations on board to be provided with VHF transceiver equipment, with October 31, 1980 as the deadline to file the necessary purchase/installation permit applications. MC 08-10-80 (October 27, 1980) had already extended that deadline once, to April 30, 1981.

Further Extension

In view of continued petitions from shipping and fishing operators for a longer compliance period, the deadline is further amended and extended, for the last time as stated in the Circular, from April 30, 1981 to December 1981. The Circular took effect immediately upon issuance.
