Executive Order No. 468 — Providing for the Creation of a National Council for the Promotion of Electronic Commerce in the Country

Status
Active
Effective Date
February 23, 1998
Signed By
President Fidel V. Ramos

Sections 1–2 — Creation and Composition

Creates a National Electronic Commerce Promotion Council to coordinate promotion and development of e-commerce, composed of the Department of Trade and Industry (Chairman), a private-sector representative (Co-Chairman), the Department of Transportation and Communication, the Department of Science and Technology, the National Economic and Development Authority, and four private-sector representatives.

Sections 3–7 — Functions, Secretariat, Funding, Repeal

The Council formulates and coordinates a National Program and Strategy for e-commerce consistent with the National Information Technology Plan, recommends policy, and provides forums on e-commerce issues. A Secretariat based at DTI provides support. DTI funds the Council's initial-year operations at P5 million, with later years sourced from private-sector and agency contributions. Inconsistent issuances are repealed or modified. Effective immediately.
