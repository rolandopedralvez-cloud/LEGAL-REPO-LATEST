Guidelines for Internet Service Providers in the Implementation of Republic Act 9775 (Anti-Child Pornography Act of 2009)

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2014-01-30
Source/Authority
RA 9775 (Anti-Child Pornography Act of 2009); RA 7925; EO 546, s. 1979

Memorandum Circular No. 01-01-2014 implements Section 3 of Republic Act No. 9775 (the Anti-Child Pornography Act of 2009) by requiring internet service providers to install filtering technology that blocks access to child pornography websites, in coordination with the Inter-Agency Council Against Child Pornography (IACACP).

Filtering technology requirements

ISPs must submit a list of at least three carrier-grade filtering technology, program, or software solutions to the IACACP for evaluation; absent an IACACP objection within five days, the ISP must acquire a solution from that list and install it to block access to child pornography websites within 120 days of that submission. NTC forwards the IACACP's list of identified pornographic websites to ISPs for immediate blocking, and ISPs are shielded from liability for complying with such blocking orders.

Reporting and data preservation duties

ISPs must report to the IACACP, within three days of each month's end, all websites their subscribers attempted to access but that were blocked. ISPs must notify the PNP or NBI within seven days of learning that child pornography is being committed using their service or facility, and must preserve customer access-time, origin, and destination records for investigation and prosecution purposes — without being required to affirmatively monitor any user's communications content.

Penalties and effectivity

Violations of the Circular are grounds for fines and other penalties under law. Any inconsistent circular, order, or memorandum is deemed repealed or amended. The Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
