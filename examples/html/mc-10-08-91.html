Memorandum Circular No. 10-8-91 — Criteria for the Grant of Commercial Radio Station Licenses

Status
Active
Issued
August 15, 1991, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended

Sets criteria for evaluating applications to establish, operate, and maintain a private commercial radio communications network, in order to conserve and effectively manage the radio frequency spectrum as a limited national resource.

Priority Services/Activities

Only private commercial radio communications networks intended for specific priority uses are allowed, including agriculture, food production and processing, animal farms, fishing, and plantations; logging, mining, forestry, and oil exploration; public utilities such as power generation and distribution, gas, water, and public telecommunications facility maintenance; land transportation such as taxis, buses, trucks, and freight services (especially for perishable goods and hazardous chemicals); and public safety services such as fire, police, paramedic, hospital ambulance, and armored car security.
