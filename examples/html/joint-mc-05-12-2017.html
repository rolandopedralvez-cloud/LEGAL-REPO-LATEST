NTC-DICT-DTI Joint Memorandum Circular No. 05-12-2017 — Prescribing a One (1) Year Expiration Period for Prepaid Loads

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2017-12-20
Source/Authority
RA 7925; RA 10844 (DICT Act of 2015); RA 7394 (Consumer Act)

Joint Memorandum Circular No. 05-12-2017, issued together by the NTC, DICT, and DTI, requires that prepaid telecommunications loads of any amount remain valid for one full year from the date of the latest top-up, replacing the tiered, amount-dependent expiration periods previously set under Memorandum Circular No. 03-07-2009.

Background and rationale

The three agencies note that, as of 2009, when NTC first set expiration periods for prepaid loads based on their carrying cost per subscriber, the subscriber base was around 75.57 million; by end-2016 it had grown to around 130 million, and since over 90% of network costs are fixed, the carrying cost per subscriber falls as the subscriber base grows. On this basis, and to give subscribers more time to use their loads, the agencies adopted a uniform one-year validity period.

Definitions

The Circular defines, among other terms, 'bucket of services' (a purchased unit of services such as calls, SMS, or data for a specific volume, value, or duration), 'prepaid service,' 'promotions,' 'public telecommunications entity,' and 'top-up.'

Scope and expiration rule

The Circular covers all prepaid loads of all ICT providers and public telecommunications entities. Prepaid loads of any amount expire one (1) year (365 days) from the date of the latest top-up; providers may offer a longer validity period but not a shorter one. Prepaid loads purchased for DTI- and/or NTC-approved promos or specific-duration buckets of services are excluded from this rule.

Violations and effectivity

Violations relating to load expiration are penalized under RA 7925, the DICT Act and its IRR, and other NTC/DICT rules; violations involving false or misleading advertising of prepaid promotions are penalized under the Consumer Act and DTI rules. The Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified copies with the UP Law Center.
