Department Order No. 7, Series of 1950 — Rules and Regulations Governing Radio Training Schools in the Philippines

Status
Amended — foundational framework, cited alongside DO 5 for radio operator training/licensing
Effective Date
July 1, 1950
Legal Basis
Act No. 3846 (Radio Control Law), as amended by Commonwealth Acts Nos. 365 and 571

Sections 1–2 — Certificate of Recognition Required

No person, firm, association, or corporation may open a radio training school without first securing a certificate of recognition from the Secretary of Commerce and Industry. Applications must state the school's name, owner, location, courses offered, instructor qualifications, equipment, and course duration.

Section 3 — Requirements for Recognition

The school owner/operator must be a Filipino citizen at least 21 years old and of good community standing (or, if a firm/corporation, at least 80% Filipino-owned). Each class is limited to fifty students under a qualified instructor.
