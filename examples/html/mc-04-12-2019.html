Guidelines for the Issuance of Deletion Certificate for the Recall of Radio Station Licenses and/or Certificates of Philippine Registered Ships

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2019-12-17
Source/Authority
Act No. 3846, as amended; EO 546, s. 1979; MC 4-2-92; MC 4-9-88

Memorandum Circular No. 04-12-2019 sets out the guidelines and requirements for obtaining a Deletion Certificate to recall the radio station license and/or certificate of a Philippine-registered ship, covering the required documents, applicable fees, and the NTC's corresponding ITU notification duty.

Application requirements

An applicant must submit a letter of request stating the reason for deletion (e.g., change of flag registry, shipbreaking/scrapping/decommissioning, or constructive/total loss), together with: a Certificate of Deletion from the Philippine Registry issued by the Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA); for vessels with Inmarsat terminals, a Certificate of Deactivation from the Point of Service Activation (PSA) Entity; and a certification from the Accounting Authority (AA) that the vessel has no outstanding maritime accounts.

Fees and post-approval obligations

Applicable fees are a Filing Fee of PHP 180.00, a Deletion Certificate Fee of PHP 200.00, and a Documentary Stamp Fee of PHP 30.00. Within ninety (90) days after approval of the Deletion Certificate, the applicant must surrender the original Radio Station License(s) and/or Certificates to the NTC.

ITU notification

The NTC, through its Safety Radio Services and STCW Compliance Division, notifies the Radio Communications Bureau of the International Telecommunications Union (ITU) of the deletion from the List of Philippine Registered Ship Stations, pursuant to ITU Radio Regulation Provision No. 20.16, addressed to the Director of the Radiocommunication Bureau in Geneva, Switzerland.

Effectivity

The Circular takes effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and the filing of three true copies with the UP Law Center.
