MC 12-09-2002 — Minimum Number of Stations for Non-Carrier Trunked Radio Communications Networks

Status
Active
Issued
2002-09-27

Pursuant to Act 3846 and in furtherance of MC 10-08-91, imposes a minimum of sixty stations per channel on applications for authority to establish, operate, and maintain a private (non-carrier) trunked radio communications network, to conserve and effectively manage spectrum.

Minimum Station Requirement and Exceptions

Requires a minimum of sixty stations per channel for private trunked radio network applications, with issued radio station licenses required to comply at all times. Networks involving national security, public safety, or overcoming man-made/natural barriers are exempted and handled case by case. Effective immediately, superseding inconsistent prior issuances.
