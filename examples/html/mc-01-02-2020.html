Source/Authority

Memorandum Circular No. 01-02-2020 further amends Section 2 (Operating Parameters) of Memorandum Circular No. 03-05-2007, restating the complete technical table of frequency bands and power/field-strength limits for non-specific short-range devices, telemetry, telecommand, alarm, and general data applications.

Updated operating parameters table

The Circular sets field-strength or output-power limits across seventeen frequency bands ranging from 6765 kHz up to 246 GHz for non-specific short-range devices, telemetry, telecommand, alarms, data, and similar applications — including 42 dBuA/m limits at the lower HF bands (6765-6795 kHz, 13.553-13.567 MHz), 10 mW erp/eirp limits across most VHF/UHF/microwave bands (26.957-27.283 MHz through 5725-5875 MHz), a 25 mW erp allowance in the 868-869.2 MHz range stepping down to 5 mW erp at 869.7-870 MHz, and 100 mW eirp allowances in the millimeter-wave bands from 24 GHz to 246 GHz.

Effect on other issuances and effectivity

Any circular, memorandum, or order inconsistent with this Circular is deemed amended or superseded. It takes effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and the filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
