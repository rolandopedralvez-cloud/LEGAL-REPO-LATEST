Memorandum Circular No. 15-12-2002 — Prescribing a Quality Standard System (QSS) in the Processing of Certificates of Competency (COC) of Marine Radio Personnel and Endorsement of Holders of Radio Electronic Certificates (REC)

Status
Active
Issued
December 15, 2002

Issued pursuant to Executive Order No. 546 series of 1979 and Act 3846, as amended, under which NTC is mandated to prescribe rules and regulations for issuing Certificates of Competency (COC) to marine radio personnel and Endorsements to holders of Radio Electronic Certificates (REC). Also references the Maritime Training Council (MTC), created under Letter of Instruction 1404 to give full effect to the 1978 STCW Convention, as amended.

General — Quality Standard System

NTC commits to implementing, complying with, and maintaining the effectiveness of its Quality Standard System (QSS) through regular internal assessment and management review, with qualified and trained personnel to implement QSS policies and procedures, in order to ensure compliance with international standards, procedures, and instructions of the International Telecommunications Union (ITU) and the International Maritime Organization (IMO).
