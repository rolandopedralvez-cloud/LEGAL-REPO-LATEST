Amending Certain Provisions of Section 2 of NTC Memorandum Circular No. 02-03-2016 (Sale and Labeling of ISDB-T Receivers)

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2017-03-27
Source/Authority
Act No. 3846, as amended; EO 546, s. 1979

Memorandum Circular No. 01-03-2017 extends the compliance timeline under Memorandum Circular No. 02-03-2016 for integrating the mandatory Emergency Warning Broadcast System (EWBS) function into ISDB-T television receivers sold in the Philippines, following consultation with the local appliance industry.

Amendments

The Circular amends three provisions of Section 2 of MC 02-03-2016: it allows ISDB-T receivers without EWBS function to remain on sale beyond the original 18-month compliance period; it restarts the 18-month manufacturer/dealer/distributor compliance window to run from completion of test-stream trials for EWBS, BML (broadcaster mark-up language), superimpose, and subtitle functions; and it sets a two-stage certification timeline — voluntary self-declaration upon this Circular's effectivity, and mandatory self-declaration 18 months after completion of the test-stream trials. The Commission retains discretion to further extend these compliance periods on the merits.

Effect on other issuances and effectivity

All other provisions of MC 02-03-2016 remain in effect. Any inconsistent circular, order, or memorandum is deemed superseded or amended. The Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
