Memorandum Circular No. 16-89A — Guidelines and Procedures for Issuance of Special Radiotelephone Operator's Permit for Fishing Vessels (SROP-FV), Without Examination, to Qualified Applicants

Status
Active
Issued
1989, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended, in line with the government's policy of simplifying procedures

Establishes guidelines for issuing a Special Radiotelephone Operator's Permit for Fishing Vessels (SROP-FV) without examination to qualified applicants — a written instrument authorizing the holder to carry out the radiotelephone service of a duly licensed radio station aboard a fishing vessel.

Scope of Authority

An SROP-FV holder may carry out only the radiotelephone service of a duly licensed radio station aboard a fishing vessel in the Philippines with power not exceeding 25 watts using F3E emission, limited to operating or adjusting external controls (on/off switch, volume, channel selection, squelch), and must operate strictly per the station license conditions.

Qualifications of Applicant

An applicant must be a Philippine citizen, of good moral character, at least 18 years old, and either the skipper/patron of, or an employee of, a firm or company authorized by the Commission to operate radio stations.
