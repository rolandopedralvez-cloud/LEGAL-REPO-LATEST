Memorandum Orders

MO 06-07-2009 — Suspension of Paragraph 4 of MC 03-07-2009 on Prepaid Load Validity

Status
Active
Issued
2009-07-15

Suspends the effectivity of Paragraph 4 of Memorandum Circular No. 03-07-2009 (Guidelines on Prepaid Loads), which required unused prepaid load balances to be added to and accumulate with a new load's validity period, after suppliers indicated a need for more time to develop and deploy the required hardware and software.

Suspended Provision and Rationale

Paragraph 4 of MC No. 03-07-2009 provided that unused load balances would accumulate with a newly purchased load's validity period (illustrated: an unused P2.00 load plus a new P10.00 load yields a combined validity of 15 days). Following consultation with software and intelligent network platform suppliers on the time needed for hardware acquisition, software development, testing, and deployment, the Commission suspends this provision's implementation until further notice, effective immediately.
