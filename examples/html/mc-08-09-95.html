Memorandum Circular No. 08-09-95 — Implementing Rules and Regulations for Republic Act No. 7925

Status
Active
Issued
September 8, 1995, by NTC, an attached agency of the Department of Transportation and Communications (DOTC)

Implements RA 7925 (the Public Telecommunications Policy Act of 1995), the law promoting and governing the development of Philippine telecommunications and the delivery of public telecommunications services. The Circular does not limit DOTC's power to monitor implementation of the Act as part of its mandate for long-term strategic national telecommunications development planning.

Definition of Terms

Defines key terms used throughout the IRR, including Access Charge, Actual Cost Approach, Cross Subsidy Approach, Interconnection Charge, Basic Telephone Service, Universal Access, Local Exchange Area, Bypass, PSTN, Pay Telephone Services (PTS), Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity (CPCN), Provisional Authority (PA), Network, Enhanced Service, Working Main Lines, and PTE (public telecommunications entity, as defined in RA 7925).

Local Exchange (LE) Services

LE operators must provide universal basic telephone service without discrimination within their authorized service areas and comply with service performance and technical standards under MC 10-17-90 and MC 10-16-90. Other PTEs generally may not extend long distance service directly to end-users within an authorized LE operator's area, except where the LE operator fails to meet response-time benchmarks (240 days in year one, 60 days in year two, 10 working days thereafter), fails to provide required interconnection within 90 days, or where compensation terms are agreed (with Commission arbitration under Rule 510 if not). A new entrant may also be authorized where an existing LE operator fails to satisfy demand, defined as serving at least 90% of applications within ten working days over the preceding three months. LE operators are entitled to fair and equitable revenue sharing under Rule 520 and are expected to use cost-effective, state-of-the-art technology.

Interexchange Carrier (IXC) and International Carrier (IC) Services

IXCs must interconnect with other IXCs and local exchange carriers on a non-discriminatory basis under Rule 510, with charges under Rule 520, and must meet the same MC 10-17-90 / MC 10-16-90 standards. International Carriers adopt the relevant provisions of the implementing guidelines on EO 109 under NTC MC 11-09-93 series of 1993, and face cancellation of their authority if they or an affiliate fail to install at least 300,000 local exchange lines (plus 300 additional lines per international switched termination beyond 1,000 terminations) and provide local exchange service within three years of receiving authority.

Mobile Radio Services

Addresses Cellular Mobile Telephone System operations, incorporating existing NTC rules and regulations on cellular mobile service as part of the broader implementation of RA 7925's policy framework for public telecommunications services.
