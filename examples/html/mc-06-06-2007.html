MC 06-06-2007 — Additional Frequency Band Allocation, 2300-2400 MHz for Broadband Wireless Access

Status
Active
Issued
2007-06-12

Pursuant to RA 7925, EO 546, and Act 3846, reallocates the 2300-2400 MHz band for broadband wireless access in addition to bands previously allocated under MC 06-08-2005, citing increasing demand for broadband wireless access.

Reallocation and Effect

Adds 2300-2400 MHz to the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table for broadband wireless access. Transfer of previously authorized stations within the band is governed by Rule 603 of MC 3-3-96. Superseded inconsistent circulars and took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
