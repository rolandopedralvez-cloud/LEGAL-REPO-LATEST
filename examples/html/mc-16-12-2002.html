Memorandum Circular No. 16-12-2002 — Prescribing a Quality Standard System (QSS) on Radio Training Centers (RTC) Offering Short-Term GMDSS Radio Communications Courses

Status
Active — companion to MC 15-12-2002 (QSS for marine radio COC/REC processing)
Issued
December 16, 2002

Issued pursuant to EO 546 series of 1979 and Act 3846, as amended, under which NTC is mandated to prescribe rules for radio training centers. As a member of the Maritime Training Council – Technical Working Group (MTC-TWG), NTC monitors the performance and quality standards of Radio Training Centers (RTCs) offering short-term GMDSS radio communications courses for seafarers, as prescribed under Regulation I/8 of the STCW-78 Convention, as amended. The Maritime Training Council itself was created under Letter of Instruction 1404 to give full effect to the STCW Convention.

General — Quality Standard System

NTC commits to ensuring implementation, compliance, and effectiveness of its Quality Standard System through regular internal assessment and management review, with qualified and trained personnel regularly assessed and requalified, so as to comply with ITU and IMO international standards when accrediting, supervising, and monitoring RTC service quality.
