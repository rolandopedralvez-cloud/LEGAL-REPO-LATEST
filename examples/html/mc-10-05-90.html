Memorandum Circular No. 10-05-90 — Sub-Allocation of Frequency Band 430-440 MHz

Status
Active — proposes rescinding MC 04-08-90
Issued
May 1990

Notes that MC 04-08-90 (February 6, 1990) had opened part of the 430-440 MHz band — previously exclusive to Amateur Radio — to commercial FX/ML use without a formal comment period, and that the Commission has since received strong representations from the amateur radio community to rescind that order.

Rationale for Proposed Rescission

Notes only 145 applications for UHF band FX/ML licenses had been received since February 1990, and cites the amateur radio community's exemplary record supporting citizens during emergencies. The Commission believes sufficient spectrum exists in the adjacent 420-430 and 440-450 MHz bands for commercial users, and that under the International Radio Regulations, any Amateur allocation of 430-440 MHz would be secondary to future commercial primary use when needed. Comments on the proposed rescission were invited by October 30, 1990, with no further commercial licenses to be issued in the band in the meantime.
