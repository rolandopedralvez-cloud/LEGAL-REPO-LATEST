MC 06-08-2005 — Frequency Band Allocations for Broadband Wireless Access

Status
Active
Issued
2005-08-23

Pursuant to RA 7925 and in support of national ICT and digital-inclusion goals, re-allocates several frequency bands for broadband wireless access to support fixed, nomadic, and mobile networks, citing the need to bridge the digital divide in unserved and underserved areas.

Reallocated Bands

Reallocates the following bands for broadband wireless access: 450-470 MHz, 1900-1910 MHz, 1980-1990 MHz, 2400-2483 MHz, 2500-2700 MHz, 3400-3600 MHz, 5150-5350 MHz, 5470-5850 MHz, and 10150-10650 MHz.

Transfer of Prior Authorizations

The transfer of previously authorized persons or entities operating radio stations within these bands is governed by Rule 603 of MC 3-3-96. Superseded inconsistent circulars and took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
