Memorandum Circular No. 07-8-83 — Issuance of Temporary Permit to Operate Radio Stations on Board Private Aircrafts and/or Pleasure Crafts to Foreigners Temporarily Stationed in the Philippines

Status
Active
Issued
1983

Allows foreign specialists, technicians, and engineers temporarily residing in the Philippines for commercial or government projects, who operate private aircraft or pleasure craft, to also operate radio stations aboard those craft, subject to conditions.

Conditions

Radio stations must be installed and operated per Act 3846, as amended, and its implementing regulations. Use is limited to safety and distress purposes only, subject to ship low-power station technical requirements, and authorized only on a frequency-sharing basis. The permit is temporary and revocable, and the applicant must be a member of a local civil aviation or maritime organization duly recognized by the Philippine government.
