MC 08-03-2000 — Form of Safety Radio Certificate for Cargo Ships

Status
Active
Issued
2000-03-01

Pursuant to the 1974 SOLAS Convention and GMDSS requirements, standardizes the form of the Cargo Ship Safety Radio Certificate issued on NTC's behalf by accredited Classification Societies, ensuring compliance with the international format.

Certificate Format Requirements

Requires the NTC Official Seal/Logo on the left portion of the Cargo Ship Safety Radio Certificate title, and the issuing Classification Society's official seal or stamp below the signature of the authorized issuing official. Effective immediately, superseding inconsistent prior issuances.
