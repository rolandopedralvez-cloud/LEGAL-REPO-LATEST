MC 4-3-96 — Operation of Spread Spectrum Technology Radios

Status
Amended
Issued
1996-03-04

Authorizes the operation of Spread Spectrum Technology radios within the 915-928 MHz, 2400-2483.5 MHz, and 5725-5850.0 MHz bands on a secondary, non-priority, non-interference basis, later implemented in detail by MC 07-06-96.

Authorized Bands and Power Limit

The 915-928 MHz band (among others) may be used for Spread Spectrum Technology on a secondary, non-priority, non-interference basis, with station output power capped at one watt effective radiated power (ERP). Operation is subject to special licensing guidelines. Effective immediately, superseding inconsistent prior issuances.
