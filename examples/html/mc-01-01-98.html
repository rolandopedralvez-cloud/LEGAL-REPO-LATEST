Memorandum Circular No. 01-01-98 — Licensing Guidelines and Procedures for Short-Range Radio Service (SRRS)

Status
Amended — later revised by MC 07-06-98
Issued
1998, pursuant to RA 7925, Act No. 3846 as amended, and EO 546

Establishes licensing guidelines and procedures for portable radio transceivers in the Short Range Radio Service (SRRS) — a private radio communication system in the Land Mobile Service limited to portable transceivers for short-range communications.

Key Definitions

An SRRS Special Permit authorizes the possession and operation of an SRRS radio station. Type Acceptance certifies SRRS equipment already type-approved by an acceptable foreign administration, based on that administration's certification and test reports, without requiring local laboratory testing. Type Approval certifies SRRS equipment complying with the Technical Standards and Specifications in Attachment A, and requires local laboratory testing.

Technical Parameters

SRRS frequency channels are allocated on a shared-usage, non-interference basis.
