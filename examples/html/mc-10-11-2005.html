Memorandum Circular No. 10-11-2005 — Guidelines on the Cablecasting of CATV Channels with "Chatroom" Formats

Status
Active
Issued
2005, pursuant to EO 205 (Regulating the Operation of Cable Antenna Television) and EO 546 series of 1979

Regulates Cable TV (CATV) "chatroom" channels — interactive channels that let viewers interact and send messages via SMS for reception by the viewing public — in furtherance of NTC's mandate to promote and protect consumer welfare.

Section 1 — Scope and Coverage

Covers all CATV stations cablecasting channels with chatroom formats and the measures required for operating those channels.

Section 2 — General Provisions

CATV stations cablecasting chatroom channels must employ an Automated Filter Dictionary (AFD) capable of automatically masking predetermined unsuitable words or phrases, previewing all messages before cablecasting, and providing the operator a facility to edit or delete messages.
