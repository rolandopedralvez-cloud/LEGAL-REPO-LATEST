MC 03-04-85 — Promulgation of Technical and Program Standards for Radio and TV Broadcast Stations

Status
Amended
Issued
1985-04-03

Pursuant to Executive Order No. 546 and Presidential Decree No. 576-A, and in recognition of the KBP's broadcast CREDO, adopts and promulgates Program Standards and Technical Standards governing the operation of radio and TV broadcast stations in the Philippines, as Annexes A and B respectively.

Two Sets of Standards

Promulgates two annexed standards: (1) Program Standards, governing broadcast content and conduct, and (2) Technical Standards, governing the technical operation of broadcast stations. Both annexes form part of this Memorandum Circular.

Subsequent Amendment

Item C of the Program Standards was later amended by Memorandum Circular No. 11-12-85, which added new paragraphs "5" and "6" addressing public service obligations and prohibitions on subversive or indecent content.
