Implementing Rules and Regulations of Memorandum Circular No. 04-06-2016 Re: Re-allocation of the 470-512 MHz Band for Digital Terrestrial Television Broadcasting (DTTB) Service in the Philippines

Type
regulation
Status
active
Effective date
2018-02-02
Source/Authority
Memorandum Circular No. 04-06-2016

Memorandum Circular No. 01-02-2018 sets out the detailed Implementing Rules and Regulations for assigning Channels 14 to 20 (the re-allocated 470-512 MHz band) to Mega Manila television networks converting from analog to Digital Terrestrial Television Broadcasting (DTTB) under Memorandum Circular No. 04-06-2016, including an eligibility hierarchy, a drawing-of-lots assignment procedure, and cost-sharing arrangements for displaced incumbent users of the band.

Allocation and eligibility tiers

Channels 14 to 20 within the 470-512 MHz band are allocated for DTTB. Eligibility follows a three-tier priority: first, all operating and duly authorized Mega Manila VHF television networks that filed the required manifestation to convert to DTTB under MC 07-12-2014; second, if channels remain unassigned, operating and duly authorized Mega Manila UHF TV networks with the same manifestation; and third, if channels still remain, entities with pending petitions for DTTB authority in Mega Manila with no existing analog operations, evaluated on legal, financial, and technical capability.

Channel assignment procedure

Eligible VHF networks are notified to submit a notice of intent (including an undertaking to share compensation costs) within set calendar-day windows; those who do not respond waive their opportunity within the 470-512 MHz band, without prejudice to a channel in the 512-698 MHz band. Assignment proceeds via a drawing of lots based on each network's stated channel preferences: a channel that is only one network's first choice is assigned automatically, while a channel chosen first by multiple networks is drawn among them, with the process repeating for second and subsequent choices until all networks are assigned. The same drawing-of-lots mechanism applies to the subsequent UHF-network and pending-petition tiers if channels remain. Assignees of Channels 14-20 keep priority for the same channels in other service areas nationwide, subject to a three-month window to apply once notified that no other channel is available.

Compensation of displaced incumbent users

The cost of transferring/replacing the radio networks of the band's prior authorized users is shared equally among all networks assigned a channel; the replacement network must be able to operate on relocation frequencies previously identified under MC No. 08-08-2007. Each channel assignee must remit PHP 9,517,060.00 to the Kapisanan ng mga Brodkasters ng Pilipinas (KBP), which holds the funds in trust and disburses them per terms set by a Technical Working Group (TWG), reporting each receipt to the Commission.

Technical Working Group and final provisions

A TWG chaired by the Commissioner or a Deputy Commissioner, with representatives from the Mega Manila VHF networks, KBP, and affected incumbent users, is created within ten (10) days of effectivity to set the Terms of Reference for the incumbent-user transfer process under MC No. 03-03-96, Rule 603. Inconsistent issuances are repealed or amended accordingly. The Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified true copies with the UP Law Center.
