MC 01-01-97 — Radio Frequency Band Allocations for Wireless Local Loop (Local Exchange Service)

Status
Active
Issued
1997-01-01

Pursuant to RA 7925 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, reallocates several frequency bands for Wireless Local Loop (WLL) use by local exchange carriers (LECs), restricting the spectrum strictly to fixed local telephone service, not mobile use.

Primary and Secondary Bands

Primary WLL bands (governed by Rule 603 of MC 3-3-96): 1880-1920 MHz, 1710-1720/1805-1815 MHz, 1850-1860/1930-1940 MHz, and 3425-3442/3475-3492 MHz. Secondary bands, available where possible on a non-interference basis: 360-365/370-375 MHz, 866-869 MHz, and 1427-1530 MHz.

Eligibility and Restrictions

Only duly enfranchised and authorized LECs may apply for WLL frequencies within their authorized service areas, with the Commission determining frequency requirements based on authorized areas, demand, and network capacity. Authorized CMTS operators may also use their CMTS frequencies for WLL within their LEC-authorized areas. Each LEC may choose any WLL technology using type-approved equipment, but the allocated spectrum is strictly for fixed local telephone service, not mobile use.
