Memorandum Circular No. 17 — Imposition of Surcharge for Late Filing of Applications for Renewal of Licenses and Permits

Status
Active — one of the earliest circulars in this repository, issued by the predecessor Radio Control Office
Issued
October 30, 1972, pursuant to Letter of Instruction No. 13-A of the President of the Philippines dated September 29, 1972

Prescribes surcharges for late filing of applications to renew radio station licenses, dealer's and manufacturer's permits, and operator licenses or permits.

Surcharge Schedule

For late renewal of station licenses and dealer's/manufacturer's permits: a 50% surcharge on the prescribed basic annual fee applies if filed within 180 days beyond expiration; a 100% surcharge applies if filed after 180 days but within 365 days of expiration. For late renewal of an operator license or permit: a 50% surcharge applies if filed within 365 days beyond expiration; filings after that period require the applicant to submit justification.
