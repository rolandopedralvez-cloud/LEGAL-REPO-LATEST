MC 10-06-99 — Amending Section E-1 of MC 7-6-98 (SRRS Special Permit Fee)

Status
Active
Issued
1999-06-01

Amends Section E No. 1 of Memorandum Circular No. 7-6-98, setting a one-time special permit fee for Short-Range Radio System (SRRS) units collected from NTC-authorized dealers.

Amended Fee

Sets a one-time special permit fee of P300.00 per SRRS unit, collected from NTC-authorized dealers upon application per Section D-1. All other provisions remain in force. Took effect fifteen (15) days after publication, superseding inconsistent prior issuances.
