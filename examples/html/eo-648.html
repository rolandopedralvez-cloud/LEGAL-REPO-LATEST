Executive Order No. 648 — Attaching the National Telecommunications Commission From the Department of Transportation and Communications (DOTC) to the Commission on Information and Communications Technology (CICT)

Status
Amended — later, EO 47 (2011) expressly retained NTC under the Office of the President as CICT was reorganized into DOST
Effective Date
August 6, 2007 (upon publication in a newspaper of general circulation)
Signed By
President Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo, by Executive Secretary Eduardo Ermita

Reciting that NTC was then attached to DOTC and invoking the President's continuing reorganization authority under the Administrative Code of 1987 (EO 292), this Order attaches NTC from DOTC to the Commission on Information and Communications Technology (CICT). Inconsistent issuances are repealed, amended, or modified accordingly. Effective immediately upon publication.
