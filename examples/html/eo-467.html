Executive Orders

Executive Order No. 467 — Providing for a National Policy on the Operation and Use of International Satellite Communications in the Country

Status
Active
Effective Date
March 17, 1998
Signed By
President Fidel V. Ramos, by Executive Secretary Alexander P. Aguirre

Section 1 — Policy Guidance

Enfranchised entities authorized by NTC to provide international telecommunications services get direct access to international fixed satellite systems, with broadcast providers also potentially allowed access subject to NTC rules. Direct access to international mobile satellite systems is allowed for maritime, aeronautical, and land mobile uses. The government takes a permissive approach to Satellite Newsgathering (SNG) earth stations operated by foreign news media for a limited period, subject to a special NTC permit. Global Mobile Personal Communication by Satellite (GMPCS) operation is allowed to support universal access, with GMPCS systems required to interconnect with terrestrial systems non-discriminatorily under EO 59 and its implementing guidelines. Direct-to-Home (DTH) TV signal reception is allowed subject to program authorization and NTC procedures for uplink centers.

Sections 2–6 — Implementation, Definitions, Review, Effectivity

NTC was directed to formulate implementing rules within 45 days. Defines Direct Access, DTH TV, Fixed Satellite Service, GMPCS, Mobile Satellite Service, and Satellite Newsgathering. DOTC is directed to periodically review the policy's responsiveness. Inconsistent issuances are repealed or modified. Effective immediately.
