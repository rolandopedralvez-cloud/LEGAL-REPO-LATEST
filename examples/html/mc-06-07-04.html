MC 06-07-2004 — Installation and Operation of Public Coast Stations

Status
Active
Issued
2004-07-20

Pursuant to Act No. 3846, EO 546, and RA 7925, sets rules for the installation, operation, and decentralized licensing of public coast stations by Public Telecommunications Entities (PTEs) to promote maritime communications and safety.

Service Area Limits

A PTE may be authorized to install and operate public coast stations in not more than two service areas per province, plus specified major cities (Cebu, Lapu-Lapu, Mandaue, Iloilo, Bacolod, Davao, Zamboanga, Cagayan de Oro, Dumaguete, Roxas, Batangas) treated separately from their provinces, and any cities/municipalities within the National Capital Region. The Commission may allow up to two HF public coast stations per service area, subject to frequency availability.

Decentralized Processing and Compliance

Processing of permit and license applications is decentralized to the Regional Office where the station is located, with no permit issued absent valid provisional authority or CPCN. Stations must operate on a non-interference basis, maintain separate HF/VHF DSC-capable receivers, and existing operators exceeding the two-station limit have one year to comply. Violations may result in suspension, modification, or cancellation of the CPCN.
