Memorandum Order No. 3-11-2005 — Guidelines for the Registration of VoIP Service Providers and Resellers

Status
Active — implements MC 05-08-2005 (VoIP)
Issued
2005

Establishes registration requirements for VoIP service providers — persons or entities providing VoIP services to the public, directly or through resellers, for compensation — and for resellers who source VoIP service from a duly registered provider to resell to retail end-users.

Registration Requirements

A VoIP service provider must submit, in addition to standard VAS registration documents, a certified true copy of SEC or DTI registration showing at least 60% Filipino ownership and paid-up capital of at least ten million pesos; a valid facilities/network lease agreement with authorized providers; valid interconnection agreements with authorized access facilities/networks (local exchange, cellular mobile, trunk radio, broadband access, etc.) as applicable under Section 4 of MC 05-08-2005; and a list of cities and municipalities where the service will be offered.
