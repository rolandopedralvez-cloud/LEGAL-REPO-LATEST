Memorandum Circular No. 09-08-91 — Guidelines in the Sale, Purchase and Importation of Various Radio Communications Equipment Intended for Use in Private and Government-Owned Radio Communications Networks

Status
Active
Issued
1991, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended

Governs the sale, purchase, and importation of radio communications equipment intended for private and government-owned radio communications networks, complementing the manufacture and sale rules under MC 2-05-88.

Importation of Radio Communications Equipment

All importations of radio transceivers and/or transmitters must be covered by a Permit to Import issued by NTC. A Permit to Import, valid for 90 days from issuance, is granted only to duly accredited radio equipment manufacturers and dealers holding a valid Radio Dealers and Manufacturer's Permit — either to import type-accepted and/or type-approved equipment and accessories as stock, or to import up to five sample units for purposes of type approval and/or acceptance testing, subject to re-export if the equipment fails evaluation.
