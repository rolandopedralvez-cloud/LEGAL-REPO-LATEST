MC 08-08-2003 — Amending Amateur Radio Call Sign Prefix Formation

Status
Active
Issued
2003-08-04

Amends Section II-2.2.1 of Memorandum Circular No. 02-03-87 (Implementation of the Revised Amateur Regulations), issued pursuant to Ministry Circular No. 87-174, to rationalize the designation of amateur radio call signs by class of operator and facilitate identification of the authorized band class.

Revised Call Sign Prefixes

Amends the call sign formation rules as follows: Class “A” uses prefix DU or 4F; Class “B” uses prefix DV; Class “C” uses prefix DW; Class “D” (no code test) uses prefix DY; and Club Stations, Repeater Stations, Special Event Stations, and DXpeditions use prefix DX or DZ.

Transition

The transition to the new prefix structure shall be made upon renewal or modification of the station license. The Circular took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette or a newspaper of general circulation and filing of three certified copies with the UP Law Center.
