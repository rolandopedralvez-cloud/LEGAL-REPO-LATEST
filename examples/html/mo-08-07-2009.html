MO 08-07-2009 — Clarifying Provisions of MC 04-07-2009 on Broadcast Messaging Service

Status
Active
Issued
2009-07-22

Provides official interpretation of specified provisions of Memorandum Circular No. 04-07-2009 (which further amended MC No. 03-03-2005A, Rules and Regulations on Broadcast Messaging Service), clarifying subscriber consent, common data platform, and network disconnection provisions.

Subscriber Consent and Consent Manifestation

Clarifies that once a subscriber completes an act of subscription (via written correspondence, text, internet, or similar means), delivery of subscribed content is no longer deemed a push message; commercial/promotional messages require prior written consent, which may be manifested through the same operative acts.

Common Platform and Disconnection Due Process

Clarifies that the "common platform" required for PTEs and content/information providers to capture data under MC No. 04-06-2007 refers to a common information/data format; and that the Commission's authority to direct disconnection of a content provider's network access, pending investigation of a subscriber complaint, requires due notice and an opportunity to be heard via a show cause order.
