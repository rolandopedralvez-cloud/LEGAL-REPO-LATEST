Memorandum Circular No. 07-06-98 — Amendments to Memorandum Circulars 02-01-97 and 01-01-98 and Providing Additional Licensing Guidelines and Procedures for Short Range Radio Service (SRRS)

Status
Active — amends MC 01-01-98 and MC 02-01-97
Issued
1998, pursuant to RA 7925, Act No. 3846 as amended, and EO 546

Amends and supplements the earlier SRRS licensing framework with additional guidelines and procedures for licensing portable radio transceivers in the Short Range Radio Service.

Definition of Terms

Restates and refines definitions for Short Range Radio Service (SRRS), SRRS Special Permit, Type Acceptance (certification based on an accepted foreign administration's type-approval certificate and test reports, without local laboratory testing), and Type Approval (certification requiring local laboratory testing against the Technical Standards and Specifications in Attachment A), along with a definition of a portable unit as a handheld device that may be conveniently moved from place to place.
