Memorandum Circular No. 07-07-86 — Implementing Procedures for the Interconnection and Operation of Cordless Telephones

Status
Active
Issued
1986, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended, and in furtherance of NTC's cordless telephone interconnection guidelines of December 29, 1982

Promulgates implementing procedures for the interconnection and operation of cordless telephone equipment with the public telephone network.

Definition of Terms

A Cordless Telephone System consists of a base radio unit connected to a standard telephone line and a battery-powered portable radiotelephone unit, allowing the portable extension to originate and receive calls at a distance from the main telephone set. A Telephone Company is an authorized grantee of a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity to install, operate, and maintain a telephone system and charge rates. A Subscriber is an individual or entity contracting for telephone company service.

Equipment Categories and Technical Specifications

Distinguishes equipment models requiring NTC type-approval from models previously type-approved, and provides that only cordless telephone equipment duly type-approved by NTC may be accepted for interconnection with a public telephone company. Sets standard technical specifications for the transmitter section, including base station frequency ranges around 1.725–1.975 MHz.
