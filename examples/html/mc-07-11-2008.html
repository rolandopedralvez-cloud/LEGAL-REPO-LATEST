MC 07-11-2008 — Amending MC 04-06-2008 on Employment of Licensed Radio Operators/Technicians

Status
Active
Issued
2008-11-04

Amends and clarifies Memorandum Circular No. 04-06-2008 regarding the requirement that radio, television, and CATV facilities employ licensed radio operators/technicians and Registered Electronics and Communications Engineers, pursuant to Department Order No. 5 (1948) and Department Order No. 88 (1973).

Clarified Requirement

Orders that all radio and TV broadcasting stations and CATV operators must comply with Department Order No. 5, s. 1948 by employing a licensed radio operator/technician at all times during operation, and that operations must be supervised by a Professional Electronics and Communications Engineer (PECE) or Registered Electronics and Communications Engineer (ECE) under Department Order No. 88, s. 1973.

Rationale and Effect

Issued because the NTC found that a number of stations and CATV operators were not employing licensed operators/technicians or ECEs. Superseded any inconsistent prior circular, memorandum, or order; took effect fifteen (15) days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.
