MC 10-09-2004 — Special Registration of Unregistered Radio Transceivers on Commercial Fishing Vessels

Status
Active
Issued
2004-01-22

Pursuant to a joint undertaking between BFAR, the Philippine Coast Guard, MARINA, and NTC (the Mobile Registration and Licensing Team), prescribes guidelines for special registration of unregistered radio transceivers on commercial fishing vessels of 3.1 gross tonnage and above, conducted alongside a national fishing-vessel inventory.

Registration Period and Scope

Registration is conducted by NTC Regional Offices from January 22, 2004 to January 22, 2005 at MARINA Regional Offices, covering radio equipment on vessels of 3.1 gross tonnage and above. Registration during this period does not itself authorize construction or operation without the required permits/licenses, and only type-approved equipment may be issued permits and licenses.

Registration Fees

Sets per-unit registration fees: HF/SSB at P5,000; VHF/UHF base/mobile low power (below 25W) at P2,000, medium power (25-100W) at P3,000, high power (above 100W) at P5,000; and VHF/UHF portable at P1,500. Registered equipment is the accountability of the registrant, with violations subject to administrative sanctions.
