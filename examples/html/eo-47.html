Executive Order No. 47 — Reorganizing, Renaming and Transferring the Commission on Information and Communications Technology and Its Attached Agencies to the Department of Science and Technology, Directing the Implementation Thereof and for Other Purposes

Status
Active
Effective Date
June 23, 2011 (upon publication)
Signed By
President Benigno S. Aquino III

Section 1 — Reorganizing and Transferring CICT to DOST

The Commission on Information and Communications Technology (CICT), created under EO 269 (s.2004), is renamed the Information and Communications Technology Office (ICTO) and placed under the policy, technical, and administrative supervision of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST). ICTO is headed by an Executive Director with the rank of Undersecretary, reporting directly to the DOST Secretary. The positions of CICT Chairman and Commissioners are abolished.

Sections 2–3 — Functions and Agency Transfers

ICTO's functions include formulating national ICT policy and programs, ensuring efficient government ICT infrastructure, conducting research with academe, building public-sector ICT capacity, and formulating the Government Information System Plan and administering the E-Governance Fund. The National Computer Center (NCC) and the Telecommunications Office (TELOF) are transferred to DOST as part of ICTO's internal structure.

Section 4 — Retention of Other Agencies Under the Office of the President

The National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) and the Philippine Postal Corporation (PHILPOST) continue to be under the Office of the President — expressly unaffected by CICT's reorganization into DOST.

Sections 5–9 — Rationalization Plan, Transitory, Repeal, Effectivity

DOST is directed to submit a rationalization plan within three months covering ICT development planning, ICTO's internal structure and staffing, budget reconfiguration, and personnel safety nets. Pending approval, unaffected personnel continue their functions. Inconsistent issuances are repealed or modified. Effective immediately upon publication in a newspaper of general circulation.
