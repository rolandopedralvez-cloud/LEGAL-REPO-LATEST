Memorandum Order No. 03-09-2018 — Advisory Regarding the Migration of GMDSS Services from Inmarsat-3 to Inmarsat-4 Satellites

Status
Superseded by events — the described migration schedule was completed by end of 2018
Issued
September 10, 2018, Quezon City

Advises Philippine-registered ship owners, operators, bareboat charterers, and ship managers of the planned migration of recognized Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS) services — Inmarsat-C and Fleet 77 — from the primary Inmarsat-3 satellites to the Inmarsat-4 satellites, based on a document submitted by the International Mobile Satellite Organization (IMSO) to the IMO Sub-Committee on Navigation, Communications and Search and Rescue.

Migration Schedule

Inmarsat-3 F5 was to remain the primary satellite for the Atlantic Ocean Region West for the time being, given its operational performance, while other Inmarsat-3 satellites would hold their orbital slots as spare capacity. Regional migrations were scheduled across 2018: Classic Aero for the Americas region migrated successfully on April 30, 2018, though a technical problem required rolling back Swift 64 and Fleet Services to the prior constellation; Pacific Ocean Region services (Classic Aero, Fleet 77, Swift 64, and Inmarsat-C) completed migration on August 29, 2018; Atlantic Ocean Region East and Indian Ocean Region migrations were targeted for the end of Q3 2018. Inmarsat committed to completing the overall migration within 2018 to avoid service disruption, with IMSO monitoring progress throughout.
