Memorandum Circular No. 01-01-02 — Mandatory Installation of NAVTEX Receiver and Satellite EPIRB as Additional Navigational Aids Equipment of Ship Radio Stations Engaged in Coastwise Trade

Status
Active
Issued
2002, pursuant to the 1974 SOLAS Convention and its amendments and Department Orders 2001-46 and 2001-47

Requires NAVTEX receivers and satellite Emergency Position Indicating Radio Beacons (EPIRBs) as additional navigational aids equipment for ship radio stations engaged in coastwise trade.

Definition of Terms

Global Positioning System (GPS) is a satellite-aided measurement system enabling a ship to determine its position anytime, anywhere. NAVTEX is a system for the broadcast and automatic reception of maritime safety information — navigational/meteorological warnings, search and rescue information, and urgent information — via narrow-band direct printing telegraphy. Satellite EPIRB is a mobile-satellite earth station that, when activated, transmits a distress unit's geographical coordinates to facilitate search and rescue via the COSPAS-SARSAT satellite-aided search and rescue system.
