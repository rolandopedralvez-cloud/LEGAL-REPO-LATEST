Republic Act No. 9775 — Anti-Child Pornography Act of 2009

Status
Active
Enacted
July 27, 2009, Fourteenth Congress

Declares State policy to guarantee fundamental rights of children against neglect, cruelty, and exploitation, and to protect them from all forms of pornographic exploitation and abuse, consistent with the Convention on the Rights of the Child and related international instruments.

Sections 3–4 — Definitions and Unlawful or Prohibited Acts

Defines "child" (including persons 18 or over unable to protect themselves due to disability, and any person depicted as a child in pornographic material) and "child pornography." Prohibits hiring, employing, coercing, or inducing a child into pornographic performance or material, producing, publishing, offering, distributing, or possessing child pornography, and using telecommunications networks or the internet to commit these acts.

Section 5 and Penalties

Syndicated child pornography — committed by a group of three or more persons — carries the penalty of reclusion perpetua and substantial fines. Individual violations carry graduated penalties depending on the specific prohibited act, up to reclusion perpetua.

Inter-Agency Council Against Child Pornography

Establishes an Inter-Agency Council against Child Pornography, empowered to promulgate implementing rules and regulations, monitor compliance, and coordinate enforcement across government agencies, including those with authority over broadcast and telecommunications content.
