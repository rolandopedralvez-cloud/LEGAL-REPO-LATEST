MC 19-11-94 — Renewal of Provisional Authorizations to Operate and Maintain CATV Systems

Status
Active
Issued
1994-10-25

In line with the government's decentralization program, decentralizes to NTC Regional Offices the filing of extensions of provisional authority (PA) to install, operate, and maintain CATV systems, while retaining the grant of permits and certificates of authority with the Central Office.

Regional Office Procedure

All petitions for PA extensions are filed with the Regional Office where the CATV system is authorized; that office must inspect the system for compliance with the PA's terms, and conduct a motu proprio inspection one month before the PA's expiration. The petition and inspection report must be forwarded to the Central Office within twenty days of filing, with the Central Office acting within ten days of receipt.

Grant of Extensions and Oversight

The Central Office may grant an extension motu proprio where there is substantial compliance; if opposed, the petition is set for hearing. Regional Offices must maintain an updated list of provisional-authority grantees and conduct ongoing ocular inspections of service adequacy. Amends any contrary circular, memorandum, or order.
