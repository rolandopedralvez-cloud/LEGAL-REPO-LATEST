Presidential Decree No. 1987 — Creating the Videogram Regulatory Board

Status
Active
Effective Date
October 5, 1985
Signed By
President Ferdinand E. Marcos

Finding that unregulated circulation of videograms (videotapes, discs, cassettes) had prejudiced movie house attendance, deprived government of tax revenue, and allowed unregulated distribution of obscene content, this Decree creates the Videogram Regulatory Board (VRB) under the Office of the President to regulate the importation, exportation, production, reproduction, distribution, exhibition, sale, lease, or disposition of videograms.

Section 2 — Composition

The Board is composed of a Chairman, Vice-Chairman, and eighteen members appointed by the President for one-year renewable terms. Members must be natural-born Filipino citizens, at least 21 years old, of good moral character, reflecting multi-sectoral videogram-industry expertise; at least five must be Bar members.

Section 3 — Powers and Functions

The Board supervises, regulates, grants, denies, or cancels permits for videogram importation, exportation, production, copying, sale, lease, exhibition, or showing, and may approve, disapprove, delete objectionable portions from, or prohibit videograms judged immoral, indecent, libelous, contrary to law or good customs, or injurious to public order — including material tending to incite subversion, insurrection, rebellion, or sedition against the State.
