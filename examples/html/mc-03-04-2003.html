MC 03-04-2003 — Revision to MC 13-11-91 Adding Foreign National Requirements for the GOC

Status
Active
Issued
2003-04-04

Amends Memorandum Circular No. 13-11-91 (General Operator's Certificate guidelines) to add a new item "f" specifying additional requirements for foreign national applicants, pursuant to Act 3846, EO 546, ITU Radio Regulations, and the 1995 STCW Convention.

Foreign Applicant Requirements

A foreign national applicant must be a licensed deck officer of a country recognized by the International Maritime Organization and must have completed the required GMDSS training course in the Philippines, submitting a photocopy of their foreign deck officer's license and photocopies of training certificates from NTC-accredited radio training centers (originals to be presented). All other provisions of MC 13-11-91 remain in force.
