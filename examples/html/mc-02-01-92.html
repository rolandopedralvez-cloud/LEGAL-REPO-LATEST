MC 02-01-92 — UHF Frequency Allocation for Personal Radio Service

Status
Active
Issued
1991-01-10

Pursuant to Memorandum Circular No. 11-21-88, allocates the UHF band 428.0 MHz to 428.5 MHz to Personal Radio Service (PRS) operators and establishes a standard 20-channel plan spaced at 0.025 MHz intervals.

Channel Plan

Defines 20 channels from Channel 1 (428.025 MHz) through Channel 20 (428.500 MHz), evenly spaced at 0.025 MHz intervals, to be assigned to PRS operators on a sharing basis.

Effect

Assignment and operation of any channel shall follow the PRS guidelines and existing radio rules and regulations, superseding inconsistent prior circulars; the Commission may review and amend it from time to time. Effective immediately.
