Presidential Decree No. 1986 — Creating the Movie and Television Review and Classification Board

Status
Active
Effective Date
October 5, 1985
Signed By
President Ferdinand E. Marcos

Citing the movie and television industry's economic difficulties and the need for a regulatory body that both reviews content and cooperates with the industry to improve it, this Decree creates the Movie and Television Review and Classification Board (MTRCB) under the Office of the President, a distinct body from NTC that governs content review and classification rather than spectrum/carrier licensing.

Section 2 — Composition and Qualifications

The Board is composed of a Chairman, Vice-Chairman, and thirty members appointed by the President for one-year terms, renewable. Members must be natural-born Filipino citizens, at least 21 years old, of good moral character, with a multi-sectoral mix of expertise; at least five members must be members of the Philippine Bar, and up to fifteen may come from the movie/television industry itself, nominated by industry associations.

Section 3 — Powers and Functions

The Board promulgates implementing rules and standards for production, advertising, and titles; screens, reviews, and classifies motion pictures and television programs (including advertisements, trailers, and stills), whether imported or locally produced, for theatrical, non-theatrical, or television distribution.
