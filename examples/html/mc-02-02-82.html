Memorandum Circular No. 02-2-82 — Implementation of SOLAS 1974

Status
Amended — its GMDSS radiocommunications provisions were later amended by MC 4-2-92
Issued
1982, referencing the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS) 1974 and the Philippines' Instrument of Accession dated October 16, 1981, and Act 3846, as amended

Prescribes guidelines and procedures for effective implementation of the SOLAS Convention 1974's telecommunications provisions, covering issuance of the Ship Safety Radio Certificate (SRC), requirements for issuance, and required additional equipment for vessels.

Definitions

Defines the Cargo Ship Safety Radiotelegraph Certificate and Cargo Ship Safety Radiotelephone Certificate, each issued after inspection to a cargo vessel with a radiotelephone installation complying with SOLAS requirements, and Classification Society, an international organization that inspects ships to safeguard life and property at sea.
