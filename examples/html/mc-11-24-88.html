MC 11-24-88 — Amending MC 04-09-88 on Shipboard Safety Radio Equipment

Status
Active
Issued
1988-01-01

Amends Memorandum Circular No. 04-09-88 (April 21, 1988), which prescribed necessary shipboard radio equipment for safety of life and property at sea, by replacing a telegraphy installation requirement with an auto watch receiver and automatic tone generating device.

Amended Equipment Requirement

Deletes the requirement for 500 KHz telegraphy installation capability on liners and cargo vessels of 350 G.T. and above with more than five hours of navigation, replacing it with an auto watch receiver and automatic tone generating device for 2182 KHz, to be complied with within 90 days. Effective immediately, amending MC 04-09-88 and any inconsistent circulars.
