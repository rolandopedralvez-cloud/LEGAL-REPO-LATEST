Memorandum Circular No. 2-1-93 — Rules and Regulations Governing the Registration and Licensing of Satellite Television Receive Only (TVRO) Stations in the Philippines

Status
Active — precedes and complements MC 10-7-93 on domestic satellite communications policy
Issued
Dated July 23, 1992, designated MC No. 2-1-93, pursuant to Act 3846, as amended, and EO 546

Establishes rules for registering and licensing Satellite Television Receive Only (TVRO) stations in the Philippines — stations that receive sound and video signals directly from a satellite.

Section 1 — Definition of Terms

Distinguishes Non-Commercial TVRO (NC-TVRO) stations — for personal use by an individual and immediate family, for government agencies/instrumentalities/offices that charge no public fees, or for foreign governments, entities, or UN agencies limited to their own premises — from Commercial TVRO (C-TVRO) stations, which operate for monetary or material gain.
