MC 15-11-2004 — Addendum to MC 17-12-2002 on Minimum Qualifications of RTC Assessors and Supervisors

Status
Active
Issued
2004-11-01

Pursuant to Act 3846, EO 546, ITU Radio Regulations, and the STCW Convention, amends Memorandum Circular No. 17-12-2002 to add minimum qualification standards for Radio Training Center (RTC) Assessors and Training/Assessment Supervisors.

RTC Assessor Qualifications

An RTC Assessor must be at least a college graduate, hold a Certificate of Assessors Course (IMO Model Course 3.12), have at least three years of practical assessment experience, hold a General Operator Certificate (GOC), and possess appropriate knowledge of GMDSS operation, equipment, and facilities.

Training/Assessment Supervisor Qualifications

A Supervisor must be at least a college graduate, familiar with the RTC's training program and instructional/training methods, hold a Certificate of Assessors Course, be knowledgeable in the STCW Convention and IMO Model Courses, and have at least five years of practical assessment experience.
