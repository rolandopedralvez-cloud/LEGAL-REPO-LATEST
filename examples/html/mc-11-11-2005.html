MC 11-11-2005 — Guidelines for Sharing/Consolidation of CATV Head-End Facilities

Status
Active
Issued
2005-11-23

Sets forth procedures allowing two or more cable television operators to share or consolidate head-end facilities to achieve economies of scale and improve subscriber service, requiring Commission approval, a Memorandum of Agreement, and a technical feasibility report.

Application and Approval Procedure

Sharing of head-end facilities requires prior Commission approval. An operator must notify the Commission of intent — grantees of a Provisional Authority or Certificate of Authority file a motion, applicants indicate it in their application or a separate motion — accompanied by a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) and a technical feasibility report demonstrating compliance with CATV technical standards under Section 5 of MC 04-08-88, including cable size requirements and signal quality.

Liability, Consolidation, and Restrictions

The MOA must assign liability for violations of NTC technical standards arising from the shared head-end. Operators serving adjoining/contiguous municipalities may consolidate into a single head-end via motion with a supporting feasibility report; all sharing operators must still maintain their respective TVRO and CATV licenses. The Commission will not approve sharing by operators who have failed to comply with their authority conditions or have pending unauthorized-retransmission cases.
