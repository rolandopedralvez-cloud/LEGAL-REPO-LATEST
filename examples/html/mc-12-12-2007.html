Memorandum Circular No. 12-12-2007 — Frequency Band Allocation for Earth Stations Located on Board Vessels (ESVs) in the Bands 5925-6425 MHz and 14-14.5 GHz on Secondary Basis

Status
Active
Issued
2007, pursuant to RA 7925 and its IRR, EO 546 series of 1979, Act No. 3846, as amended, and Rule 600 of MC 8-9-95

Allocates the 5925-6425 MHz and 14-14.5 GHz bands for use by earth stations located on board vessels (ESVs) on a secondary basis, in accordance with ITU frequency allocations.

Secondary Service Allocation

As a secondary service, ESV stations in these bands must not cause harmful interference to stations of primary services already assigned frequencies (or that may be assigned later), and cannot claim protection from harmful interference caused by primary service stations.
