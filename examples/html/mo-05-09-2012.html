Memorandum Order No. 05-09-2012 — Emergency Radio Communications Protocol for Radio Volunteers (NTC Task Force Bayanihan)

Status
Active
Issued
September 8, 2012, Quezon City

Prescribes standard operating procedures for amateur radio and civic action group volunteers supporting emergency, disaster, and calamity response, and sets communications and messaging protocols to ensure timely, accurate, and correct message handling, including continuous training and a repository of radio communication messages.

Radio Communications Network

Establishes an NTC Radio Control Center (RCC) where net control of Amateur and Civic Action Group volunteers reports during emergencies. The RCC maintains direct linkages with the Metro Manila Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (MMDRRMC) and coordinates with agencies such as MMDA, the Office of Civil Defense, and the DILG, acting as the overall coordinator of radio messages from volunteer groups and assigning emergency frequencies.

Information Validation and Standard Operating Procedures

Information from MMDRRMC, PAGASA, or PHIVOLCS is treated as official and need not be validated; information from other sources must be verified by MMDRRMC before dissemination. During activation, the NTC RCC calls stations to stand by, requests check-ins from affected areas, and controls radio traffic in order of priority (emergency, priority, welfare traffic), using only plain language and a prescribed message format (callsign and club affiliation, addressee/originator, contact verification, body of message, sign-off). Emergency traffic records must be kept by participating operators for five years.

Repealing, Saving, and Effectivity Clauses

All inconsistent circulars, memoranda, or orders are repealed or modified accordingly. If any part is found unlawful or unconstitutional, the remaining parts stand on their own. The Order took effect immediately upon issuance.
