Memorandum Circular No. 02-06-2006 — Guidelines for the Grant and Authorization of 500 Watt FM Broadcast Stations

Status
Active
Issued
2006, pursuant to EO 546 series of 1979 and Act 3846, as amended

Allows FM broadcast stations to operate with transmitters of less than the standard 1 kilowatt minimum required for Class B stations, at 500 watts, in order to enable localities to have broadcast service focused on local or community news, issues, and concerns.

Rationale

Notes NTC's authority under EO 546 to promulgate rules encouraging a larger and more effective use of communications and broadcasting facilities while maintaining effective competition, and that the country's geography and terrain, together with the government's goal of providing wider access to news, entertainment, information, and education to unserved and underserved areas, make lower-power transmitters necessary and desirable for local broadcasting.
