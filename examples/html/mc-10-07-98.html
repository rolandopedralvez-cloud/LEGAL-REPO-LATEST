MC 10-7-98 — Rules and Guidelines Implementing STCW-95 for Radio Personnel and Training Schools

Status
Amended
Issued
1998-07-20

Pursuant to ITU Radio Regulations, the 1995 STCW Convention, and Department Order No. 7, promulgates comprehensive rules and guidelines for radio personnel and Radio Training Schools (RTS) offering GMDSS radiocommunication courses, covering GMDSS certificate issuance, examiner qualifications, RTS quality standards, and course curricula.

GMDSS Certification and Examiner Standards

NTC issues GMDSS certificates (valid up to five years) to radio personnel who pass its written/practical examinations; renewal requires at least one year of shipping company employment certification. Examiners must be registered Electronics and Communications Engineers with GMDSS training and at least five years of regulatory experience implementing SOLAS/STCW.

RTS Quality Standards and Curriculum

An accredited Radio Training School must hold a valid Radio Station License and Certificate of Recognition (each valid up to five years), maintain graduate pass-rate records, and face suspension if graduates fall below a 70% NTC exam pass rate. REC, GOC, and ROC courses must cover subject matter specified in MC Nos. 6-6-98, 13-11-91, 8-7-98, and 9-7-98 respectively, with curriculum details in Annexes A and B aligned to STCW Code Chapter IV requirements.
