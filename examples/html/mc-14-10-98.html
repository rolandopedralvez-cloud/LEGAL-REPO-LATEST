MC 14-10-98 — Renewal/Extension of Temporary Permits for Broadcast Operators Without a Legislative Franchise

Status
Active
Issued
1998-10-01

Implements a May 3, 1994 Memorandum of Understanding between NTC, the House Committee on Legislative Franchises, and the KBP, setting a firm deadline and conditions for radio/TV broadcast operators who have not yet secured a legislative franchise to retain their temporary operating permits.

Deadline and Filing Requirement

Broadcast operators without a legislative franchise are given until December 31, 1999 to secure Congressional approval of a franchise bill, which must be filed no later than November 30, 1998. Affected operators must file for renewal/extension of their Temporary Permits together with certification from the House Committee on Legislative Franchises that a franchise bill was filed by that date.

Consequence of Non-Compliance

If a permittee's franchise bill is not approved within the prescribed period, NTC will not renew or extend the Temporary Permit and will initiate recall of the assigned frequency, subject to due process. No new Certificate of Public Convenience application will be accepted without proof of an approved legislative franchise. Published requirement, taking effect thirty (30) days after publication.
